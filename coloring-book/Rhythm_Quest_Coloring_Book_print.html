Staying on the path. Always learning.
Before you begin

Be the hero of your own learning.

Inside are 38 pages from the Rhythm Quest — the lands, the friends, and the animals your child meets along the path. Print them one at a time or all at once. There is no wrong color and no wrong order.

Say it out loud

Read the line at the top of each page together before the crayons come out.

Take your time

One page a day is plenty. Slow hands build steady focus.

Hang it up

Finished pages belong on the wall. Every one is a step on the path.

Designed for the developing brain — not the algorithm.
