## 2. Three-Phase Budget Phasing & Scaling Framework

### Phase 1: Rapid Angle & Creative Testing (Weeks 1–2)
* **Daily Budget:** **$15 – $25 / day**
* **Campaign Structure:** **ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization)**
* **Ad Sets (3–4 sets at $5/day each):**
  - **Ad Set 1 (Broad + Demographics):** Parents with toddlers (2–4) & early elementary (5–7), US only.
  - **Ad Set 2 (Conscious & Screen-Free):** Montessori, Waldorf, Screen-free parenting, Nature play.
  - **Ad Set 3 (Arts & Early Learning):** Kindermusik, Suzuki Method, Dalcroze, Early Childhood Education.
* **Creatives per Ad Set:** 3–4 ads from Wave 1 (Ad 03 Before/After, Ad 04 Contrarian, Ad 09 PAS, Ad 21 One-Line Knockout).
* **Primary Metric:** Cost per Email Lead (CPL) on `/listen` (<$1.75) and Outbound CTR (>1.8%).

### Phase 2: Optimization & Lookalike Incubation (Weeks 3–6)
* **Daily Budget:** **$40 – $75 / day**
* **Campaign Structure:** **CBO (Advantage+ Campaign Budget)** + Dedicated Retargeting ABO
* **Budget Split:**
  - **80% ($35–$60/day) TOFU Cold Scaling:**
    - Winning creatives consolidated into CBO.
    - Targeting: **1% Lookalike of `/listen` Leads** + Top 2 Interest Stacks.
  - **20% ($10–$15/day) MOFU/BOFU Retargeting:**
    - Targeting: 30-day website visitors + 75% video viewers + email non-buyers.
    - Creatives: Ad 05 Challenge, Ad 13 Social Proof, Ad 26 Vulnerability.

### Phase 3: High-Scale Expansion & Global Concord (Weeks 7+)
* **Daily Budget:** **$100 – $250+ / day**
* **Campaign Structure:** **Advantage+ Shopping / Broad CBO**
* **Audience Stacking:**
  - Stacked Lookalikes (1%–5% Leads + Purchasers).
  - Open Broad Targeting (Meta AI demographic matching based on post-engagement signals).
  - Geographic expansion: US, Canada, UK, Australia, plus dedicated bilingual parent targeting for French (`fr.json`) and international markets.

## 6. KPI Benchmarks & Automated Decision Rules

### Target Benchmarks
* **Outbound Click-Through Rate (CTR):** `> 1.80%` (Top performers `> 2.50%`)
* **Cost Per Outbound Click (CPC):** `< $0.85 – $1.25`
* **Cost Per Lead (CPL) on `/listen`:** `< $1.25 – $1.75`
* **Tripwire Conversion Rate (from lead to $19/$35 purchase):** `3.5% – 7.0%`
* **Blended Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** `> 2.8x – 3.5x`

### Automated Kill & Scale Rules

| Trigger Condition | Automated Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CTR < 1.0% after 500 impressions** | **Kill/Pause Creative.** The hook failed to stop the scroll. |
| **CPL > 2x Target ($3.50+) after 1,000 impressions** | **Kill Ad Set.** Retargeting or audience saturation issue. |
| **CPL < $1.40 and ROAS > 3.0x for 48 consecutive hours** | **Scale Budget +20%.** Increment campaign budget without resetting the learning phase. |
| **Ad frequency exceeds 3.5 within 7 days in MOFU** | **Refresh Creative.** Swap image asset or test secondary hook archetype. |
