# SOE YouTube Ads Placement Targeting & Campaign Blueprint

> **Strategy Objective:** Intercept high-intent, screen-fatigued parents and preschool educators on YouTube at the exact moment they search for or play hyper-stimulating toddler entertainment. Manage and scale via Google Ads Video Action Campaigns (VAC) optimized for direct card vaulting ($0 Front End $\rightarrow$ $7 Bump $\rightarrow$ $35 Workbook).

---

## 1. Campaign Structure & Bidding Configuration

| Setting | Configuration |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Campaign Type** | Video Action Campaign (VAC) / YouTube In-Stream Skippable & In-Feed |
| **Bidding Strategy** | Target CPA ($8.50 – $14.00 during test phase; $6.50 target at scale) |
| **Target Audience** | Parents of Preschoolers (Ages 2–3) and Parents of Early Elementary (Ages 4–7) |
| **Parental Demographics** | Ages 25–44, Top 40% Household Income |
| **Device Targeting** | Mobile + Tablet + Connected TV (Exclude Unknown / Embedded App Webviews) |
| **Conversion Goals** | Primary: Direct Card Vaulting ($7 Starter Pack Bump or $21/$35 Workbook Purchase). Secondary: Lead Unlock (`listen_direct_optin`). |

## 4. Placement Exclusions (Negative Filtering)

To prevent wasted ad spend on algorithmic gaming and older children:
- **Gaming & Minecraft Channels** (Roblox, Minecraft, DanTDM, Unspeakable)
- **Tween & Teen Entertainment** (MrBeast, Ryan's World, Cartoon Network, Nickelodeon)
- **Foreign Language Nursery Rhymes** (unless deploying to dedicated Spanish/French campaigns)
- **Non-Educational Toy Unboxing Videos**
