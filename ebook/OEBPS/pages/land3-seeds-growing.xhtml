Scene 20: Seeds & Growing

In Vesta's Greenhouse, every seed tells a story

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Seed n. /SEED/ Plant the seed in the soil and water it. Mime sprinkling seeds from pinched fingers
2 Sprout n. /SPROWT/ The sprout pushes up through the soil. Fingerspell SPROUT
3 Seedling n. /SEED-ling/ The seedling needs sunlight to grow strong. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Root n. /ROOT/ Roots grow underground and drink water. 5-hand pushes down through opposite C-hand
5 Stem n. /STEM/ The stem holds the flower up toward the sun. Fingerspell STEM
6 Leaf n. /LEEF/ Each leaf catches sunlight. Index finger hangs from opposite wrist, sways
7 Bud n. /BUD/ A bud is a flower before it opens. Fingerspell BUD
8 Bloom n. /BLOOM/ The flowers bloom in spring. Fingerspell BLOOM
9 Petal n. /PET-ul/ Each petal of the rose is soft and colorful. Fingerspell PETAL
10 Pollen n. /POL-en/ Bees carry pollen from flower to flower. Fingerspell POLLEN
11 Compost n. /KOM-pohst/ Add compost to make soil richer. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Fertilizer n. /FUR-tih-ly-zer/ Fertilizer helps plants grow bigger. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Greenhouse n. /GREEN-hows/ Grow plants year-round in the greenhouse. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Water v. /WOT-er/ Water the plants every morning. W-hand taps chin
15 Sunlight n. /SUN-lyt/ Plants need sunlight to make food. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Soil n. /SOYL/ Healthy soil is dark and rich. Fingerspell SOIL
17 Pot n. /POT/ Plant the herb in a small clay pot. P-hands mime stirring in pot
18 Planter n. /PLAN-ter/ The window planter is full of flowers. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Weed n. /WEED/ Pull the weeds so they do not steal nutrients. Fingerspell WEED
20 Harvest v. /HAR-vest/ Harvest the tomatoes when they turn red. Curved hand mimes gathering crops
21 Vine n. /VYN/ The vine climbs up the garden fence. Fingerspell VINE
22 Branch n. /BRANCH/ The bird sits on a branch of the oak tree. Fingerspell BRANCH
23 Trunk n. /TRUNK/ The trunk of the tree is thick and strong. Fingerspell TRUNK
24 Bark n. /BARK/ The bark protects the tree like a coat. Fingerspell BARK
25 Growth n. /GROHTH/ The growth of the plant was amazing. Fingerspell GROWTH
