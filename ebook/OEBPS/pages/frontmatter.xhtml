How to Use Your Quest Dictionary

An interactive roadmap for young explorers, families, and educators

👑 Seriphia’s 5-Step Quest for Young Explorers

Welcome, Rhythm Explorer! You and your grown-up are on a quest through 7 magical Lands. Here is how to master every page:

1
🔍
Spot It First

Look at the big illustration. Find the numbered markers (1, 2, 3...) and solve the green Quest Spot-It Challenge!

2
🎵
Scan & Sing

Tap or scan the Scan & Sing button at the top of the Land to hear the companion song on soelearn.com/listen.

3
👂
Sound Before Symbol

Say the word aloud using the syllable guide. Check the Sound Spotlight to clap the beat!

4
🏷️
Word Classes

Learn the word's job with color badges: n. Noun (thing), v. Verb (action), adj. (describing).

5
✍️
Claim Your Language

Practice the ASL handshape 🤟, then write the word in your family’s home language in the blank line!

For Families: Shared Reading

This dictionary is designed for parents and children to explore together. Each page features a thematic scene from one of the 7 Lands of the Rhythm Quest. Point to each numbered item in the illustration, say the word aloud, then find it in the vocabulary table below.

Reading the Vocabulary Table & Badges

# The item number matching the illustration callout
Word & Badge The English vocabulary word with its grammatical word class (n. noun, v. verb, adj. adjective)
/Phonetic/ Pronunciation guide using natural syllable breaks and capitalized stressed syllables
In the Story… A narrative sentence showing how the word connects to the SOE characters and world
ASL Sign 🤟 Physical handshape description for American Sign Language inclusion
My Language A dedicated blank line to write the translation in your heritage or home language

For Teachers & Programs (Title III & Common Core)

This dictionary is aligned with the thematic scope of the Oxford Picture Dictionary and supports Title III (English Language Acquisition) programming and state early learning standards (Head Start ELOF, NAEYC, and Common Core ELA Language Standards L.K.1, L.1.1, L.2.1). Each Land maps to standard ESOL survival vocabulary topics. The sub-scenes can be used as standalone lesson units.

💡 Print Referencing Tip

When reading with a child, point to each word as you say it. Tap the number in the illustration, then trace your finger to the matching number in the table. Use the vertical edge ribbon on the right to practice alphabetizing and navigating between the 7 Lands. This builds the crucial connection between auditory phonology, physical gesture, and written literacy.
