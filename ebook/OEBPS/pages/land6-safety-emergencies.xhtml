Scene 5: Safety & Emergencies

In the Sophia Safety Center, Ezra and Athena learn the words that can save a life.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Emergency n. /ih-MUR-jun-see/ In an emergency, call 911 immediately. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Danger n. /DAYN-jer/ The "Danger" sign warns you to stay away. Fingerspell DANGER
3 Caution n. /KAW-shun/ Use caution when crossing the busy street. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Warning n. /WOR-ning/ The warning light flashes when there is a problem. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Exit n. /EK-sit/ Find the nearest exit in case of emergency. Fingerspell EXIT
6 Fire escape n. /FYR es-KAYP/ Use the fire escape if the stairs are blocked. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Fire extinguisher n. /FYR ek-STING-gwish-er/ The fire extinguisher puts out small fires. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Smoke detector n. /SMOHK dih-TEK-ter/ The smoke detector beeps loudly when it senses smoke. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 First aid n. /FURST AYD/ First aid is immediate help before the doctor arrives. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 CPR n. /SEE-PEE-AR/ CPR can save a life by keeping blood flowing. Fingerspell CPR
11 Poison n. /POY-zun/ Keep poison and chemicals away from children. Fingerspell POISON
12 Hazard n. /HAZ-erd/ A wet floor is a slipping hazard. Fingerspell HAZARD
13 Evacuate n. /ih-VAK-yoo-ayt/ Evacuate the building calmly and quickly. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Rescue n. /RES-kyoo/ Firefighters rescue people from burning buildings. Fingerspell RESCUE
15 Safe adj. /SAYF/ Stay in a safe place during a storm. S-hands cross at wrists, then pull apart
16 Helmet n. /HEL-met/ Wear a helmet when riding a bicycle. Both C-hands come down on head
17 Seat belt n. /SEET BELT/ Always buckle your seat belt in the car. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Life jacket n. /LYF JAK-it/ Wear a life jacket when you are on a boat. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Crosswalk n. /KROS-wawk/ Use the crosswalk to cross the street safely. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Stop, drop, and roll n. /STOP DROP and ROHL/ If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop, and roll. Fingerspell or sign each word
🚨

Ezra's Tip:

"Safety words can SAVE YOUR LIFE. Memorize your address and phone number in English. Practice: 'My address is ___. My phone number is ___. I need HELP.' You are always safer when you can communicate."
