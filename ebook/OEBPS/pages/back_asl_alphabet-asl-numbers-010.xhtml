Scene 3: ASL Numbers 0–10

Numbers are everywhere — now learn to show them with your hands!

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Zero n. /ZEER-oh/ Zero means nothing or none. O-hand shape
2 One n. /WUN/ One is a single item. Index finger up
3 Two n. /TOO/ Two means a pair. Index and middle up
4 Three n. /THREE/ Three comes after two. Thumb, index, and middle up
5 Four n. /FOR/ Four fingers but no thumb. Four fingers up, thumb folded
6 Five n. /FYV/ Five is a whole hand. All five fingers spread open
7 Six n. /SIKS/ Six is five plus one. Pinky meets thumb, three fingers up
8 Seven n. /SEV-en/ Seven days in a week. Ring finger meets thumb, three up
9 Eight n. /AYT/ Eight legs on a spider. Middle finger meets thumb, three up
10 Nine n. /NYN/ Nine is almost ten. Index meets thumb, four fingers up
11 Ten n. /TEN/ Ten fingers, ten toes. A-hand (thumb up), shake
