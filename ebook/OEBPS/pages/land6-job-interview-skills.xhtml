Scene 15: Job Interview Skills

At the Sophia Career Center, Dara practices for the big interview

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Interview n. /IN-ter-vyoo/ Prepare for the job interview. I-hands alternate near mouth
2 Resume n. /REZ-oo-may/ Submit your resume with the application. Fingerspell RESUME
3 Qualifications n. /kwol-ih-fih-KAY-shunz/ List your qualifications on your resume. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Experience n. /ek-SPEER-ee-ents/ She has five years of work experience. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Reference n. /REF-er-ents/ Provide three references who know your work. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Strengths n. /STRENGTHS/ My strengths include teamwork and communication. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Weakness n. /WEEK-nes/ Describe a weakness and how you overcame it. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Punctual n. /PUNK-choo-ul/ Be punctual and arrive five minutes early. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Professional n. /proh-FESH-un-ul/ Dress in professional clothes for the interview. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Handshake n. /HAND-shayk/ Give a firm handshake when you meet the interviewer. Clasp hands together, mime shaking
11 Eye contact n. /EYE KON-takt/ Make eye contact when answering questions. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Confident n. /KON-fih-dent/ Speak in a confident and clear voice. Both C-hands pull down from chest firmly
13 Nervous adj. /NUR-vus/ It is normal to feel nervous before an interview. Both 5-hands shake in front
14 Hire n. /HYR/ The company will hire the best candidate. H-hand sweeps in toward body
15 Position n. /poh-ZISH-un/ What position are you applying for? Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Salary n. /SAL-er-ee/ The salary for this job is competitive. Flat hand scoops off opposite palm toward body
17 Benefits n. /BEN-ih-fits/ Benefits include health insurance and vacation. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Full-time n. /FULL-tym/ A full-time job is forty hours per week. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Part-time n. /PART-tym/ She works part-time while attending school. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Shift n. /SHIFT/ The morning shift starts at seven. Fingerspell SHIFT
21 Background check n. /BAK-grownd CHEK/ The employer runs a background check. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Follow up n. /FOL-oh UP/ Follow up with a thank-you email. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Offer n. /OF-er/ She received a job offer on Friday. Fingerspell OFFER
24 Accept n. /ak-SEPT/ He decided to accept the position. Fingerspell ACCEPT
25 Decline n. /dih-KLYN/ She had to decline the offer. Fingerspell or sign each word
