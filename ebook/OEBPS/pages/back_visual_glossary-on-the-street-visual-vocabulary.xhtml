Scene 3: On the Street — Visual Vocabulary

Step outside your door and the world becomes a picture dictionary!

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Sidewalk n. /SYD-wawk/ Walk on the sidewalk, not in the street. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Crosswalk n. /KROS-wawk/ Use the crosswalk to cross the street safely. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Traffic light n. /TRAF-ik LYT/ Stop at the red traffic light. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Stop sign n. /STOP SYN/ Come to a complete stop at the stop sign. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Parking meter n. /PAR-king MEE-ter/ Put coins in the parking meter. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Fire hydrant n. /FYR HY-drant/ Never park in front of a fire hydrant. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Streetlight n. /STREET-lyt/ The streetlight turns on automatically at dusk. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Bench n. /BENCH/ Sit on the bench and wait for the bus. Fingerspell BENCH
9 Manhole n. /MAN-hohl/ Workers go underground through the manhole cover. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Telephone pole n. /TEL-eh-fohn POHL/ Power lines hang between the telephone poles. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Intersection n. /in-ter-SEK-shun/ The accident happened at the intersection. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Pedestrian n. /peh-DES-tree-un/ The pedestrian waited for the walk signal. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Curb n. /KURB/ Step off the curb carefully. Fingerspell CURB
14 Gutter n. /GUT-er/ Rainwater flows into the gutter. Fingerspell GUTTER
15 Speed bump n. /SPEED BUMP/ Slow down at the speed bump. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Overpass n. /OH-ver-pas/ The highway overpass crosses above the local road. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Tunnel n. /TUN-ul/ The train goes through the tunnel under the mountain. Fingerspell TUNNEL
18 Bridge n. /BRIJ/ Cross the bridge to get to the other side of the river. V-hand bridges under opposite forearm
19 Billboard n. /BIL-bord/ The billboard advertises a new movie. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Mailbox n. /MAYL-boks/ Drop the letter in the blue public mailbox. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Newspaper stand n. /NOOZ-pay-per STAND/ Buy a newspaper at the newspaper stand. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Awning n. /AW-ning/ Stand under the awning to stay dry in the rain. Fingerspell AWNING
23 Alley n. /AL-ee/ The alley runs between two buildings. Fingerspell ALLEY
24 Playground n. /PLAY-grownd/ Children play on the swings at the playground. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Fountain n. /FOWN-tun/ Toss a coin into the fountain and make a wish. Fingerspell or sign each word
