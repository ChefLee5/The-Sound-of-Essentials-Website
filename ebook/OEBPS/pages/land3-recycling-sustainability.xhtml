Scene 15: Recycling & Sustainability

Silas and Vesta teach everyone in Terrasol how to protect the land for future generations.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Recycle n. /ree-SY-kul/ Recycle paper, plastic, and glass. R-hand circles (going around again)
2 Reuse n. /ree-YOOZ/ Reuse the bag instead of throwing it away. Fingerspell REUSE
3 Reduce n. /rih-DOOS/ Reduce waste by buying only what you need. Fingerspell REDUCE
4 Trash n. /TRASH/ Put trash in the trash can, not on the ground. Fingerspell TRASH
5 Garbage n. /GAR-bij/ Garbage trucks pick up the bins every week. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Litter n. /LIT-er/ Never litter — always find a proper bin. Fingerspell LITTER
7 Bin n. /BIN/ The blue bin is for recycling. Fingerspell BIN
8 Compost n. /KOM-pohst/ Compost turns food scraps into rich soil. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Landfill n. /LAND-fil/ Trash goes to the landfill when it can't be recycled. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Pollution n. /puh-LOO-shun/ Pollution makes the air and water dirty. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Clean energy n. /KLEEN EN-er-jee/ Solar and wind are forms of clean energy. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Solar panel n. /SOH-ler PAN-ul/ A solar panel turns sunlight into electricity. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Wind turbine n. /WIND TUR-byn/ The wind turbine spins to make electricity from wind. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Water bottle n. /WAW-ter BOT-ul/ Use a reusable water bottle instead of plastic. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Plastic n. /PLAS-tik/ Too much plastic ends up in the ocean. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Paper n. /PAY-per/ Recycle paper to save trees. Both flat hands brush past each other
17 Glass n. /GLAS/ Glass can be recycled again and again. C-hand rises from opposite palm (tall glass)
18 Aluminum n. /uh-LOO-mih-num/ Aluminum cans are easy to recycle. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Conserve n. /kun-SURV/ Conserve water by turning off the faucet. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Protect n. /proh-TEKT/ It is our job to protect the environment. Fingerspell or sign each word

Silas's Tip:
