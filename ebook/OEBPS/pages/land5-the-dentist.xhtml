Scene 4: The Dentist

Amara visits Dr. Sona's dental office in Vitalis. Her teeth are checked, cleaned, and polished sparkling white.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Dentist n. /DEN-tist/ The dentist examines Amara's teeth carefully. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Dental hygienist n. /DEN-tul hy-JEE-nist/ The dental hygienist cleans Amara's teeth with special tools. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Dental chair n. /DEN-tul CHAIR/ Amara leans back in the comfortable dental chair. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Tooth n. /TOOTH/ Each tooth has a different shape and job. Fingerspell TOOTH
5 Molar n. /MOH-ler/ The flat molar in the back grinds food into small pieces. Fingerspell MOLAR
6 Canine n. /KAY-nyn/ The pointed canine tooth tears food apart. Fingerspell CANINE
7 Incisor n. /in-SY-zer/ The sharp incisor at the front bites into an apple. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Gum n. /GUM/ Healthy gums are pink and firm around each tooth. Fingerspell GUM
9 Cavity n. /KAV-ih-tee/ A cavity is a small hole caused by sugar and plaque. Fingerspell CAVITY
10 Filling n. /FIL-ing/ The dentist puts a filling in the cavity to fix it. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Crown n. /KROWN/ A crown covers and protects a damaged tooth. Fingerspell CROWN
12 Braces n. /BRAY-siz/ Braces straighten teeth slowly over time. Fingerspell BRACES
13 Retainer n. /rih-TAY-ner/ A retainer keeps teeth in their new position after braces. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Dental X-ray n. /DEN-tul EKS-ray/ The dental X-ray shows what's happening under the gums. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Fluoride n. /FLOR-eyed/ Fluoride treatment makes teeth stronger. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Plaque n. /PLAK/ Plaque is a sticky film of bacteria on the teeth. Fingerspell PLAQUE
17 Floss n. /FLOS/ Use floss to clean between your teeth every day. Fingerspell FLOSS
18 Toothbrush n. /TOOTH-brush/ Amara brushes with a soft toothbrush twice a day. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Toothpaste n. /TOOTH-payst/ Mint toothpaste makes your mouth feel fresh and clean. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Mouthwash n. /MOWTH-wash/ Swish mouthwash around for thirty seconds, then spit. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Dental mirror n. /DEN-tul MIR-er/ The dental mirror lets the dentist see the back teeth. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Drill n. /DRIL/ The small drill removes the decayed part of the tooth. Fingerspell DRILL
23 Numbing gel n. /NUM-ing JEL/ Numbing gel prevents pain during the procedure. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Rinse n. /RINS/ Rinse your mouth with water after the cleaning. Fingerspell RINSE
25 Wisdom tooth n. /WIZ-dum TOOTH/ Wisdom teeth usually appear in the late teens. Fingerspell or sign each word

Amara's Tip:

"How do you take care of your teeth? Say these three sentences: 'I brush my teeth. I floss my teeth. I visit the dentist.' Now YOU are in charge of your smile!"
