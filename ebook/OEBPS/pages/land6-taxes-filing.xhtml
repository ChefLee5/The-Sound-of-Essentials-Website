Scene 18: Taxes & Filing

At the Sophia Tax Office, Dara learns that taxes support community

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Tax n. /TAKS/ Pay your income tax every year. T-hand or X-hand taps opposite palm
2 Tax return n. /TAKS rih-TURN/ File your tax return by April fifteenth. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Refund n. /REE-fund/ You may get a refund if you overpaid taxes. Fingerspell REFUND
4 W-2 n. /DUB-ul-yoo TOO/ Your employer sends a W-2 form each January. Fingerspell W-2
5 Income n. /IN-kum/ Report all income on your tax return. Hand scoops money toward body
6 Deduction n. /dih-DUK-shun/ A deduction reduces the tax you owe. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Dependent n. /dih-PEN-dent/ Claim your children as dependents. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Filing status n. /FY-ling STAT-us/ Choose your filing status: single or married. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Federal n. /FED-er-ul/ Federal taxes go to the national government. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 State tax n. /STAYT TAKS/ State tax rates vary by state. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Sales tax n. /SAYLZ TAKS/ Sales tax is added at the register. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Property tax n. /PROP-er-tee TAKS/ Property tax is based on home value. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Audit n. /AW-dit/ An audit reviews your tax records. Fingerspell AUDIT
14 Receipt n. /rih-SEET/ Keep receipts for tax deductions. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Accountant n. /uh-KOWN-tent/ Hire an accountant to prepare your taxes. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 IRS n. /EYE-AR-ES/ The IRS collects federal taxes. Fingerspell IRS
17 Extension n. /ek-STEN-shun/ File an extension if you need more time. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Withholding n. /with-HOHL-ding/ Withholding is tax taken from your paycheck. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Exempt n. /eg-ZEMPT/ Some items are exempt from sales tax. Fingerspell EXEMPT
20 Penalty n. /PEN-ul-tee/ A penalty is charged for late filing. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Gross pay n. /GROHS PAY/ Gross pay is your total earnings before taxes. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Net pay n. /NET PAY/ Net pay is what you take home after taxes. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Tax bracket n. /TAKS BRAK-it/ Your tax bracket determines your rate. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Volunteer tax help n. /VOL-un-TEER TAKS HELP/ Free volunteer tax help is available. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 E-file n. /EE-fyl/ E-file your tax return for faster processing. Fingerspell E-FILE
