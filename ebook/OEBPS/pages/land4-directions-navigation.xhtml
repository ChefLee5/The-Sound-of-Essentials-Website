Scene 6: Directions & Navigation

Ronan and Nerissa walk through the streets of a new city, asking for and giving directions.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Left n. /LEFT/ Turn left at the traffic light. Fingerspell LEFT
2 Right n. /RYT/ Turn right after the grocery store. R-hand rises (right/entitlement)
3 Straight n. /STRAYT/ Go straight for two blocks. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Turn n. /TURN/ Turn at the next corner. Fingerspell TURN
5 Block n. /BLOK/ The library is three blocks away. Fingerspell BLOCK
6 Corner n. /KOR-ner/ Meet me at the corner of Main and Oak Street. Fingerspell CORNER
7 Across from n. /uh-KROS FRUM/ The bank is across from the park. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Next to n. /NEKST TOO/ The pharmacy is next to the grocery store. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Between prep. /bih-TWEEN/ The school is between the library and the park. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Behind prep. /bih-HYND/ The parking lot is behind the building. Fingerspell BEHIND
11 In front of prep. /IN FRUNT UV/ The bus stop is in front of the hospital. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Near prep. /NEER/ The restaurant is near the train station. Fingerspell NEAR
13 Far prep. /FAR/ The airport is far from the city center. Fingerspell FAR
14 Above prep. /uh-BUV/ The apartment is above the shop. Fingerspell ABOVE
15 Below prep. /bih-LOH/ The parking garage is below the mall. Fingerspell BELOW
16 Inside prep. /in-SYD/ Go inside the building and turn left. Fingerspell INSIDE
17 Outside prep. /owt-SYD/ Wait outside the entrance for the group. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Upstairs n. /UP-stairz/ The restroom is upstairs on the second floor. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Downstairs n. /DOWN-stairz/ The café is downstairs in the basement. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 GPS n. /JEE-PEE-ES/ Use the GPS on your phone to find the way. Fingerspell GPS
21 Landmark n. /LAND-mark/ The clock tower is a helpful landmark for navigation. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Location n. /loh-KAY-shun/ Share your location so I can find you. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Lost n. /LOST/ I'm lost. Can you help me find the bus station? Fingerspell LOST
24 Detour n. /DEE-toor/ Take a detour because the road is closed. Fingerspell DETOUR
25 Dead end n. /DED END/ The road is a dead end — turn around. Fingerspell or sign each word

Nerissa's Tip:

"Give directions from your seat right now! Say: 'The door is on my LEFT. The window is on my RIGHT. The teacher is IN FRONT OF me.' You just learned prepositions by looking around the room!"
