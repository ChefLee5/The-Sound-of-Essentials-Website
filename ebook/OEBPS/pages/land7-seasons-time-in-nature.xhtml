Scene 13: Seasons & Time in Nature

The Garden of Time blooms and fades with the turning seasons. Nature keeps its own clock.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Spring n. /SPRING/ Flowers bloom and baby animals are born in spring. Fingerspell SPRING
2 Summer n. /SUM-er/ Summer brings long, hot days and school vacation. Fingerspell SUMMER
3 Fall n. /FAWL/ In fall, the leaves change color and drop from trees. Fingerspell FALL
4 Winter n. /WIN-ter/ Winter is the coldest season with short days. Fingerspell WINTER
5 Season n. /SEE-zun/ A season is one of four parts of the year. Fingerspell SEASON
6 Harvest v. /HAR-vest/ Harvest is the time when farmers pick their crops. Curved hand mimes gathering crops
7 Bloom n. /BLOOM/ Roses bloom in late spring and early summer. Fingerspell BLOOM
8 Migrate n. /MY-grayt/ Birds migrate south when winter comes. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Hibernate n. /HY-ber-nayt/ Bears hibernate through the entire winter. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Sunrise n. /SUN-ryz/ Sunrise is earlier in summer and later in winter. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Sunset n. /SUN-set/ The sunset paints the sky orange and pink. Fingerspell SUNSET
12 Annual n. /AN-yoo-ul/ An annual event happens once every year. Fingerspell ANNUAL
13 Monthly adv. /MUNTH-lee/ Rent is a monthly expense — paid every month. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Weekly adv. /WEEK-lee/ The weekly schedule is posted on the bulletin board. Fingerspell WEEKLY
15 Daily adv. /DAY-lee/ Daily exercise keeps your body and mind strong. Fingerspell DAILY
16 Hourly adv. /OW-er-lee/ The bus runs on an hourly schedule. Fingerspell HOURLY
17 Cycle n. /SY-kul/ The seasons move in a cycle, year after year. Fingerspell CYCLE
18 Change n. /CHAYNJ/ Nature teaches us that change is constant. A-fists switch positions (swap)
19 Rhythm n. /RITH-um/ The rhythm of the seasons guides all living things. R-hand bounces forward rhythmically
20 Eternal n. /ee-TUR-nul/ The Garden of Time reminds us that time is eternal. Fingerspell or sign each word

Elias & Selene's Tip:

"Time moves in circles — like the SEASONS! Say: 'In SPRING I ___. In SUMMER I ___. In FALL I ___. In WINTER I ___.' Fill in YOUR activities and you've just described your year!"
