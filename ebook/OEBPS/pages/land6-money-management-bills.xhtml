Scene 7: Money Management & Bills

Ezra and Athena sit at the kitchen table in Sophia, organizing their monthly expenses.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Bill n. /BIL/ Pay the electricity bill before the due date. Fingerspell BILL
2 Rent n. /RENT/ Rent is the monthly payment for your apartment. Fingerspell RENT
3 Mortgage n. /MOR-gij/ A mortgage is a monthly payment for a house. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Utilities n. /yoo-TIL-ih-teez/ Utilities include water, gas, and electricity. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Electricity n. /ee-lek-TRIS-ih-tee/ The electricity bill goes up in the summer. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Water bill n. /WAW-ter BIL/ The water bill is paid every two months. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Gas bill n. /GAS BIL/ The gas bill is higher in the winter for heating. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Insurance n. /in-SHOOR-uns/ Health insurance helps pay for doctor visits. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Car insurance n. /KAR in-SHOOR-uns/ Car insurance is required by law in most states. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Due date n. /DOO DAYT/ Pay before the due date to avoid a late fee. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Late fee n. /LAYT FEE/ A late fee is charged if you pay after the due date. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Income n. /IN-kum/ Income is the total money you earn. Hand scoops money toward body
13 Expense n. /ek-SPENS/ Rent is the biggest monthly expense. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Budget n. /BUJ-it/ A budget tracks what you earn and what you spend. B-hands on opposite palm (MONEY + PLAN)
15 Debt n. /DET/ Pay off your debt as quickly as you can. Fingerspell DEBT
16 Credit score n. /KRED-it SKOR/ A high credit score helps you get better loan rates. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Minimum payment n. /MIN-ih-mum PAY-ment/ Always pay at least the minimum payment on time. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Paycheck n. /PAY-chek/ The paycheck arrives every two weeks. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Direct deposit n. /dih-REKT dih-POZ-it/ Direct deposit puts money straight into your bank. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Savings n. /SAY-vingz/ Put ten percent of every paycheck into savings. Both hands scoop toward body (gathering)
🧾

Ezra's Tip:

"Adulting in English means knowing money words. Practice: 'I pay RENT. I pay my BILLS. I have a BUDGET. I put money in SAVINGS.' These are power sentences for grown-up life!"
