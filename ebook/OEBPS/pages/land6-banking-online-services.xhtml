Scene 17: Banking & Online Services

At the Sophia Digital Bank, Dara manages money online

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Account n. /uh-KOWNT/ Open a checking account at the bank. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Deposit n. /dih-POZ-it/ Deposit your paycheck into savings. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Withdraw n. /with-DRAW/ Withdraw cash from the ATM. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Transfer n. /TRANS-fer/ Transfer money between your accounts. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Balance n. /BAL-ents/ Check your account balance online. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Statement n. /STAYT-ment/ Review your monthly bank statement. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 ATM n. /AY-TEE-EM/ The ATM is outside the bank. Fingerspell ATM
8 PIN n. /PIN/ Never share your PIN with anyone. Fingerspell PIN
9 Password n. /PAS-wurd/ Create a strong password for online banking. P-hand taps opposite palm (SECRET + WORD)
10 Mobile app n. /MOH-bul AP/ Use the mobile app to check your balance. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Direct deposit n. /dih-REKT dih-POZ-it/ Set up direct deposit to get paid faster. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Check n. /CHEK/ Write a check to pay the landlord. Fingerspell CHECK
13 Wire transfer n. /WYR TRANS-fer/ A wire transfer sends money electronically. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Savings account n. /SAY-vingz uh-KOWNT/ A savings account earns interest. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Checking account n. /CHEK-ing uh-KOWNT/ Use your checking account for daily expenses. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Credit card n. /KRED-it KARD/ Pay with a credit card and pay the balance monthly. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Debit card n. /DEB-it KARD/ A debit card takes money directly from your account. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Loan n. /LOHN/ Apply for a loan to buy a car. Fingerspell LOAN
19 Mortgage n. /MOR-gij/ A mortgage is a loan to buy a house. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Payment n. /PAY-ment/ Make your car payment on time every month. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Due date n. /DOO DAYT/ The payment due date is the fifteenth. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Overdue n. /OH-ver-doo/ The bill is overdue by two weeks. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Late fee n. /LAYT FEE/ Avoid the late fee by paying on time. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Fraud n. /FROD/ Report fraud to your bank immediately. Fingerspell FRAUD
25 Security n. /seh-KYOOR-ih-tee/ Online security protects your personal information. Fingerspell or sign each word
