Scene 7: The Produce Market

Felix and Amara visit the outdoor Vitalis Produce Market every Saturday morning. The stalls overflow with colorful fruits and vegetables.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Apple n. /AP-ul/ Felix picks a crisp red apple from the basket. X-knuckle twists on cheek
2 Banana n. /buh-NAN-uh/ The yellow banana is a perfect snack for energy. Mime peeling banana from index finger
3 Orange adj. /OR-inj/ Amara peels an orange and shares the sections. C-hand squeezes near chin (like squeezing orange)
4 Grape n. /GRAYP/ Purple grapes grow in clusters on the vine. Fingerspell GRAPE
5 Strawberry n. /STRAW-bair-ee/ The sweet strawberry is small, red, and juicy. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Blueberry n. /BLOO-bair-ee/ Tiny blueberries are packed with vitamins. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Watermelon n. /WAW-ter-mel-un/ A big slice of watermelon cools you down in summer. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Pineapple n. /PYN-ap-ul/ The pineapple has a rough outside and sweet inside. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Mango n. /MANG-goh/ The ripe mango is soft, sweet, and bright orange. Fingerspell MANGO
10 Peach n. /PEECH/ The fuzzy peach is a favorite summer fruit. Fingerspell PEACH
