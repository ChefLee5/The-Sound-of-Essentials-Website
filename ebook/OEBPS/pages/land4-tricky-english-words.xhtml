Scene 9: Tricky English Words

The Aquaria Word Lab is where Ronan and Nerissa study the trickiest, most confusing words in English — the words that sound alike but mean different things.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Their / There / They're n. /THAIR/ Their house is there. They're coming soon. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Your / You're n. /YOR / YOOR/ Your book is here. You're doing great! Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Its / It's n. /ITS / ITS/ The dog wags its tail. It's a sunny day. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 To / Too / Two n. /TOO/ Go to the store. I want to come too. Buy two apples. Fingerspell or sign each word

9 Ate / Eight n. /AYT/ I ate eight grapes at lunch. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 See / Sea n. /SEE/ I can see the sea from the top of the hill. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Weather / Whether n. /WETH-er/ Check the weather to know whether to bring an umbrella. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Accept / Except n. /ak-SEPT / ek-SEPT/ I accept everything except the last offer. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Affect / Effect n. /uh-FEKT / ih-FEKT/ The rain will affect the game. The effect is a wet field. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Then / Than n. /THEN / THAN/ First eat, then play. She is taller than he is. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Loose / Lose n. /LOOS / LOOZ/ The loose button may cause you to lose it. Fingerspell or sign each word

17 Advice / Advise n. /ad-VYS / ad-VYZ/ I give advice. I advise you to study. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Borrow / Lend n. /BOR-oh / LEND/ Can I borrow your pen? Can you lend me your pen? Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Bring / Take n. /BRING / TAYK/ Bring your book here. Take your book there. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Learn / Teach n. /LURN / TEECH/ Students learn. Teachers teach. Fingerspell or sign each word

Ronan's Tip:

"English has so many tricky words! The secret? Use them in sentences. 'THEIR dog is THERE. THEY'RE playing.' Write each one five times and the confusion disappears!"
