Nerissa and Reef dine at the Aquaria Seaside Cafe

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Menu n. /MEN-yoo/ Look at the menu to choose your meal. Fingerspell MENU
2 Appetizer n. /AP-eh-ty-zer/ Start with an appetizer before the main course. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Entree n. /ON-tray/ The entree is the main dish of the meal. Fingerspell ENTREE
4 Dessert n. /dih-ZURT/ Save room for dessert after dinner. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Beverage n. /BEV-er-ij/ Would you like a beverage with your meal? Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Waiter n. /WAY-ter/ The waiter takes your order at the table. Fingerspell WAITER
7 Hostess n. /HOH-stis/ The hostess seats you at your table. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Reservation n. /rez-er-VAY-shun/ Make a reservation for seven o'clock. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Order n. /OR-der/ Are you ready to order? Fingerspell ORDER
10 Specials n. /SPESH-ulz/ The daily specials are on the board. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Check n. /CHEK/ May I have the check, please? Fingerspell CHECK
12 Tip n. /TIP/ Leave a fifteen percent tip for good service. Fingerspell TIP
13 Takeout n. /TAYK-owt/ Order takeout if you want to eat at home. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Drive-through n. /DRYV-throo/ The drive-through is open late. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Buffet n. /buh-FAY/ The buffet has many choices. Fingerspell BUFFET
16 Booth n. /BOOTH/ Sit in the booth by the window. Fingerspell BOOTH
17 Napkin n. /NAP-kin/ Place the napkin on your lap. Fingerspell NAPKIN
18 Silverware n. /SIL-ver-wair/ The silverware includes a fork, knife, and spoon. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Refill n. /REE-fil/ May I have a refill on my water? Fingerspell REFILL
20 Allergies n. /AL-er-jeez/ Tell the waiter about any food allergies. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Portion n. /POR-shun/ The portion size is very generous. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Gratuity n. /gruh-TOO-ih-tee/ Gratuity is included for large groups. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Side dish n. /SYD DISH/ Choose a side dish with your entree. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 To go n. /too GOH/ I would like my coffee to go, please. Fingerspell TO GO
25 Compliment n. /KOM-plih-ment/ Compliment the chef on the delicious meal. Fingerspell or sign each word
