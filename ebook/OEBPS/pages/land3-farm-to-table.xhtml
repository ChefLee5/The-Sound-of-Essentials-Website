Scene 10: Farm to Table

In Terrasol, everyone knows where their food comes from. Silas and Vesta tend their crops through every season.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Plow n. /PLOW/ The farmer uses a plow to turn the soil before planting. Fingerspell PLOW
2 Plant v. /PLANT/ Vesta plants the seeds in neat, straight rows. P-hand pushes up through opposite C-hand
3 Harvest v. /HAR-vest/ The harvest happens in the fall when the crops are ripe. Curved hand mimes gathering crops
4 Irrigation n. /ir-ih-GAY-shun/ Irrigation brings water from the river to the fields. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Orchard n. /OR-cherd/ Apple and pear trees fill the sunny orchard. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Grain n. /GRAYN/ Golden grain waves in the wind. Fingerspell GRAIN
7 Wheat n. /WEET/ Wheat is ground into flour to make bread. Fingerspell WHEAT
8 Corn n. /KORN/ Tall corn stalks grow in rows as high as Silas's head. Fingerspell CORN
9 Rice n. /RYS/ Rice paddies need lots of water to grow. Both cupped hands scoop up
10 Cotton n. /KOT-un/ Soft white cotton is picked from the plant. Fingerspell COTTON
11 Soybean n. /SOY-been/ Soybeans are used to make tofu and soy sauce. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Crop n. /KROP/ This year's corn crop is the best in ten years. Fingerspell CROP
13 Field n. /FEELD/ The wide green field stretches to the horizon. F-hand circles outward (open area)
14 Barn n. /BARN/ The harvested grain is stored in the barn. Fingerspell BARN
15 Sickle n. /SIK-ul/ In old times, farmers cut wheat with a curved sickle. Fingerspell SICKLE
16 Scythe n. /SYTH/ The long scythe cuts tall grass with each swing. Fingerspell SCYTHE
17 Basket n. /BAS-kit/ Vesta fills a woven basket with ripe tomatoes. Fingerspell BASKET
18 Crate n. /KRAYT/ Wooden crates of vegetables are loaded onto the truck. Fingerspell CRATE
19 Market n. /MAR-kit/ Silas and Vesta sell their produce at the village market. Both flat O-hands flick out from body
20 Season n. /SEE-zun/ Each season brings different crops to the farm. Fingerspell SEASON
21 Spring planting n. /SPRING PLAN-ting/ Spring planting begins when the ground thaws. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Summer growing n. /SUM-er GROH-ing/ Summer growing means long days of sunshine and water. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Fall harvest n. /FAWL HAR-vest/ The fall harvest is a celebration of hard work. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Winter rest n. /WIN-ter REST/ Winter rest lets the soil recover for the next year. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Organic n. /or-GAN-ik/ Organic farming uses no chemicals on the crops. Fingerspell or sign each word
26 Compost n. /KOM-pohst/ Food scraps become compost to feed the soil. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 Drought n. /DROWT/ A drought means no rain for a very long time. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Fertile n. /FUR-tul/ The dark, fertile soil is perfect for growing vegetables. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Livestock n. /LYV-stok/ Cows, pigs, and chickens are all livestock. Fingerspell or sign each word
30 Beekeeper n. /BEE-keep-er/ The beekeeper collects sweet honey from the hives. Fingerspell or sign each word
31 Pollination n. /pol-ih-NAY-shun/ Bees help with pollination so plants can make fruit. Fingerspell or sign each word
32 Sprinkler n. /SPRING-kler/ The sprinkler waters the garden in a spinning circle. Fingerspell or sign each word
33 Tiller n. /TIL-er/ The tiller breaks up the hard soil before planting. Fingerspell TILLER
34 Sow n. /SOH/ In spring, farmers sow seeds in the prepared fields. Fingerspell SOW
35 Reap n. /REEP/ To reap means to cut and gather the ripe crop. Fingerspell REAP
