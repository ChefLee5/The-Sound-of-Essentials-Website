Scene 1: Essential Sight Words — Group A

Every language has words that appear everywhere. These are the 50 most common words in English — the building blocks of every sentence.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 The n. /THUH/ The sun rises over Harmonia every morning. Fingerspell THE
2 Is n. /IZ/ Felix is a doctor at the Vitalis clinic. Fingerspell IS
3 Are n. /AR/ The children are playing in the park. Fingerspell ARE
4 Was n. /WUZ/ The weather was sunny yesterday. Fingerspell WAS
5 Were n. /WUR/ Ezra and Athena were at the library. Fingerspell WERE
6 Have n. /HAV/ I have two brothers and one sister. Bent hands touch chest
7 Has n. /HAZ/ She has a new job at the hospital. Fingerspell HAS
8 Had n. /HAD/ He had breakfast before leaving for school. Fingerspell HAD
9 Do n. /DOO/ What do you do for work? Fingerspell DO
10 Does n. /DUZ/ Does the bus stop here? Fingerspell DOES
11 Did n. /DID/ Did you finish your homework? Fingerspell DID
12 Will n. /WIL/ I will go to the store after school. Flat hand at cheek moves forward
13 Would n. /WOOD/ Would you like some water? Fingerspell WOULD
14 Can n. /KAN/ I can speak two languages. S-fists push down firmly
15 Could n. /KOOD/ Could you help me, please? Fingerspell COULD
16 Should n. /SHOOD/ You should eat breakfast every morning. X-hand bends down from shoulder repeatedly
17 Must n. /MUST/ You must wear a seat belt in the car. X-hand bends down firmly once
18 May n. /MAY/ May I use the bathroom? Fingerspell MAY
19 Might n. /MYT/ It might rain this afternoon. Fingerspell MIGHT
20 Not n. /NOT/ I do not understand the question. Fingerspell NOT
21 And n. /AND/ Bread and butter are on the table. Fingerspell AND
22 But n. /BUT/ I like math, but I love science. Fingerspell BUT
23 Or n. /OR/ Do you want coffee or tea? Fingerspell OR
24 If n. /IF/ If it rains, bring an umbrella. Fingerspell IF
25 Then n. /THEN/ First study, then play. Fingerspell THEN
26 Because n. /bih-KUZ/ I stayed home because I was sick. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 So n. /SOH/ It was cold, so I wore a jacket. Fingerspell SO
28 Very n. /VAIR-ee/ The test was very difficult. Fingerspell VERY
29 Also n. /AWL-soh/ She speaks Spanish and also English. Fingerspell ALSO
30 Just n. /JUST/ I just finished eating dinner. Fingerspell JUST
