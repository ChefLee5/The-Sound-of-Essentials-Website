Scene 12: Mental Health & Emotions

In Vitalis, the heart matters as much as the body. Felix and Amara learn to name feelings and care for their minds.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Stress n. /STRES/ Deep breathing helps reduce stress. Fingerspell STRESS
2 Anxiety n. /ang-ZY-uh-tee/ Anxiety is when you feel worried about many things. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Calm adj. /KAHM/ Yoga and music help Felix feel calm. Both flat hands push down slowly
4 Relax n. /rih-LAKS/ Take a break and relax your mind and body. Fingerspell RELAX
5 Mindfulness n. /MYND-ful-nes/ Mindfulness means paying attention to the present moment. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Self-care n. /SELF-KAIR/ Self-care includes rest, exercise, and healthy eating. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Therapy n. /THAIR-uh-pee/ Therapy is talking to a professional about your feelings. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Counselor n. /KOWN-sel-er/ A school counselor helps students solve problems. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Support n. /suh-PORT/ Friends and family give you emotional support. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Resilience n. /rih-ZIL-yuns/ Resilience means bouncing back after a hard time. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Gratitude n. /GRAT-ih-tood/ Writing a gratitude list helps you see the good things. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Confidence n. /KON-fih-dens/ Practice builds confidence in speaking English. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Overwhelmed n. /oh-ver-WELMD/ Amara feels overwhelmed when there's too much to do. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Meditation n. /med-ih-TAY-shun/ Meditation means sitting quietly and focusing on breathing. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Well-being n. /WEL-BEE-ing/ Well-being includes feeling good in body AND mind. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Cope n. /KOHP/ Exercise and music help Felix cope with hard days. Fingerspell COPE
17 Journal n. /JUR-nul/ Writing in a journal helps you understand your feelings. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Patience n. /PAY-shuns/ Learning a new language requires patience. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Self-esteem n. /SELF-es-TEEM/ High self-esteem means believing in yourself. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Rest v. /REST/ Your mind needs rest just as much as your body. Arms cross over chest (resting)
