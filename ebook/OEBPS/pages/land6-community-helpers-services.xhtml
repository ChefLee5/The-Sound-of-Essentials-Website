Scene 9: Community Helpers & Services

The people of Sophia take care of each other. Every helper has a title and a task.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Firefighter n. /FYR-fy-ter/ Call a firefighter if you see smoke or fire. B-hand at forehead (shield)
2 Paramedic n. /pair-uh-MED-ik/ A paramedic arrives quickly in an ambulance. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Police officer n. /puh-LEES OF-ih-ser/ A police officer helps keep the community safe. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Mail carrier n. /MAYL KAIR-ee-er/ The mail carrier delivers letters to every house. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Garbage collector n. /GAR-bij kuh-LEK-ter/ The garbage collector picks up trash every week. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Crossing guard n. /KROS-ing GARD/ The crossing guard helps children cross the street safely. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Librarian n. /ly-BRAIR-ee-un/ The librarian helps you find the right book. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Coach n. /KOHCH/ The coach teaches teamwork and sportsmanship. C-hand taps shoulder (CAPTAIN)
9 Tutor n. /TOO-ter/ The volunteer tutor helps adults learn English. Fingerspell TUTOR
10 Counselor n. /KOWN-sel-er/ A counselor listens and helps you work through problems. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Interpreter n. /in-TUR-preh-ter/ An interpreter translates spoken words in real time. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Case worker n. /KAYS WUR-ker/ The case worker helps families access social services. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Dispatcher n. /dis-PACH-er/ The 911 dispatcher sends help to the right location. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Lifeguard n. /LYF-gard/ The lifeguard watches swimmers at the pool. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Security guard n. /sih-KYOOR-ih-tee GARD/ A security guard monitors the building entrance. Fingerspell or sign each word

Ezra's Tip:

"Community helpers are everywhere! Next time you see one, say: 'Thank you!' in English. 'Thank you, Officer. Thank you, Mail Carrier. Thank you, Librarian.' Gratitude is a language that everyone speaks."
