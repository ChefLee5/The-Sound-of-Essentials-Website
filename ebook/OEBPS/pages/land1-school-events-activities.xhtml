Scene 17: School Events & Activities

The Harmonia School Calendar is packed with exciting events all year long!

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Assembly adv. /uh-SEM-blee/ The whole school meets in the gym for assembly. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Field trip n. /FEELD TRIP/ Our field trip to the zoo is next Wednesday. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Science fair n. /SY-ents FAIR/ Aiko won first place at the science fair. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Talent show n. /TAL-ent SHOH/ Students sing and dance at the talent show. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Picture day n. /PIK-chur DAY/ Wear your nicest clothes for picture day. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Spirit week n. /SPIR-it WEEK/ During spirit week, everyone wears school colors. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Book fair n. /BOOK FAIR/ Buy new books at the school book fair. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Fire drill n. /FYR DRIL/ During a fire drill, walk quickly to the exit. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Pep rally adv. /PEP RAL-ee/ The pep rally gets everyone excited for the game. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Graduation n. /graj-oo-AY-shun/ Graduation is a proud day for students and families. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Award n. /uh-WORD/ She received an award for perfect attendance. Fingerspell AWARD
12 Certificate n. /ser-TIF-ih-kit/ The certificate shows you completed the program. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Ceremony n. /SAIR-eh-moh-nee/ The ceremony will be held in the auditorium. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Auditorium n. /aw-dih-TOR-ee-um/ The auditorium has seats for five hundred people. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Stage n. /STAYJ/ The actors perform on the stage. Fingerspell STAGE
16 Cafeteria n. /kaf-eh-TEER-ee-uh/ We eat lunch in the cafeteria every day. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Gymnasium n. /jim-NAY-zee-um/ The basketball game is in the gymnasium. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Recess n. /REE-ses/ Recess is the best part of the school day! Fingerspell RECESS
19 Semester n. /seh-MES-ter/ Each school year has two semesters. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Yearbook n. /YEER-book/ Sign my yearbook on the last day of school! Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Clubs n. /KLUBZ/ Join clubs to make friends who share your interests. Fingerspell CLUBS
22 Team n. /TEEM/ Our soccer team won the championship. Fingerspell TEAM
23 Coach n. /KOHCH/ The coach teaches the team how to play. C-hand taps shoulder (CAPTAIN)
24 Captain n. /KAP-tin/ The team captain leads warm-ups before practice. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Tryout n. /TRY-owt/ Tryouts for the basketball team are on Monday. Fingerspell TRYOUT
