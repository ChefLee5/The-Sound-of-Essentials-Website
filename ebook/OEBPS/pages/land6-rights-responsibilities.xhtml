Scene 10: Rights & Responsibilities

In the Sophia Town Hall, Athena leads a workshop on what it means to be a responsible citizen.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Right n. /RYT/ You have the right to speak freely. R-hand rises (right/entitlement)
2 Responsibility n. /rih-spon-sih-BIL-ih-tee/ Paying taxes is a responsibility of every citizen. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Duty n. /DOO-tee/ Jury duty is serving on a jury when called. Fingerspell DUTY
4 Respect v. /rih-SPEKT/ Respect other people's opinions and beliefs. R-hand arcs from forehead outward
5 Equal n. /EE-kwul/ All people are created equal. Both bent hands tap together evenly
6 Fair n. /FAIR/ A fair system treats everyone the same. Fingerspell FAIR
7 Obey n. /oh-BAY/ Citizens must obey the law. Fingerspell OBEY
8 Protect n. /proh-TEKT/ It is everyone's duty to protect the environment. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Serve n. /SURV/ Many people serve their community through volunteer work. Fingerspell SERVE
10 Cooperate n. /koh-OP-er-ayt/ Cooperate with others to solve big problems. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Participate n. /par-TIS-ih-payt/ Participate in school events and community meetings. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Honest n. /ON-est/ Being honest means telling the truth. Fingerspell HONEST
13 Tolerance n. /TOL-er-uns/ Tolerance means accepting people who are different from you. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Diversity n. /dih-VUR-sih-tee/ Diversity means having people of many backgrounds together. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Inclusion n. /in-KLOO-zhun/ Inclusion means making sure everyone feels welcome. Fingerspell or sign each word
🌍

Athena's Tip:

"Big words, big ideas! Say: 'I believe in EQUALITY. I practice TOLERANCE. I value DIVERSITY.' These words describe the world we want to build — together."
