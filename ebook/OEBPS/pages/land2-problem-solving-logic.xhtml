In the Numeria Puzzle Room, Kwame and Octavia work through puzzles that test reasoning, patterns, and logic.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Problem n. /PROB-lem/ Read the problem carefully before you solve it. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Solution n. /soh-LOO-shun/ Every problem has at least one solution. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Strategy n. /STRAT-uh-jee/ A strategy is a plan for solving a problem. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Step n. /STEP/ Solve the problem one step at a time. Fingerspell STEP
5 Clue n. /KLOO/ The clue in the sentence helps find the answer. Fingerspell CLUE
6 Pattern n. /PAT-ern/ Find the pattern: 2, 4, 6, 8 — what comes next? Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Sort n. /SORT/ Sort the shapes by color and size. Fingerspell SORT
8 Group n. /GROOP/ Group the animals that live in water. Fingerspell GROUP
9 Order n. /OR-der/ Put the numbers in order from smallest to largest. Fingerspell ORDER
10 Classify n. /KLAS-ih-fy/ Classify the objects as living or nonliving. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Compare n. /kum-PAIR/ Compare the two circles — which is bigger? Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Contrast n. /kun-TRAST/ Contrast the summer and winter temperatures. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Cause n. /KAWZ/ The cause of the flood was heavy rain. Fingerspell CAUSE
14 Effect n. /ih-FEKT/ The effect of the sun is warmth and light. Fingerspell EFFECT
15 Puzzle n. /PUZ-ul/ Kwame enjoys solving a difficult puzzle. Fingerspell PUZZLE
16 Reason n. /REE-zun/ Give a reason for your answer. Fingerspell REASON
17 Evidence n. /EV-ih-dents/ Show evidence from the text to support your answer. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Conclusion n. /kun-KLOO-zhun/ The conclusion summarizes what you learned. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Hypothesis n. /hy-POTH-ih-sis/ A hypothesis is your best guess before testing. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Experiment n. /ek-SPER-ih-ment/ An experiment tests whether your hypothesis is correct. Fingerspell or sign each word
