Scene 14: Rocks & Minerals

Deep in the Terrasol Cavern, Silas and Vesta discover a world of sparkling stones and ancient formations.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Rock n. /ROK/ Silas picks up a smooth rock from the riverbed. Fingerspell ROCK
2 Stone n. /STOHN/ The garden path is lined with flat stones. Fingerspell STONE
3 Pebble n. /PEB-ul/ A pebble is a very small, smooth rock. Fingerspell PEBBLE
4 Boulder n. /BOHL-der/ The giant boulder sits at the bottom of the hill. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Sand n. /SAND/ The sand on the beach is warm between your toes. Fingerspell SAND
6 Gravel n. /GRAV-ul/ Gravel crunches under your feet on the path. Fingerspell GRAVEL
7 Clay n. /KLAY/ Vesta shapes the wet clay into a bowl. Fingerspell CLAY
8 Crystal n. /KRIS-tul/ The purple crystal sparkles in the cave light. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Mineral n. /MIN-er-ul/ A mineral is a natural substance found in the earth. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Fossil n. /FOS-ul/ The fossil shows a fern leaf from millions of years ago. Fingerspell FOSSIL
11 Gem n. /JEM/ A polished gem shines like a tiny star. Fingerspell GEM
12 Quartz n. /KWORTS/ Quartz is one of the most common minerals. Fingerspell QUARTZ
13 Marble n. /MAR-bul/ The smooth marble feels cool in your hand. Fingerspell MARBLE
14 Granite n. /GRAN-it/ The kitchen counter is made of hard granite. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Limestone n. /LYM-stohn/ Limestone is used to build strong walls. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Lava n. /LAH-vuh/ Hot lava flows down the side of a volcano. Fingerspell LAVA
17 Magma n. /MAG-muh/ Magma is melted rock deep inside the earth. Fingerspell MAGMA
18 Erosion n. /ih-ROH-zhun/ Erosion is when wind or water wears away rock. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Sediment n. /SED-ih-ment/ Layers of sediment build up on the river bottom. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Stalactite n. /stuh-LAK-tyt/ A stalactite hangs down from the ceiling of the cave. Fingerspell or sign each word
