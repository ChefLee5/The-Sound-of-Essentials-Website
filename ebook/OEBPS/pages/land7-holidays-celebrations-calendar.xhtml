Scene 16: Holidays & Celebrations Calendar

The Celestia Festival Calendar marks every special day

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 New Year n. /NOO YEER/ Happy New Year! January first is a fresh start. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Valentine's Day n. /VAL-en-tynz DAY/ Exchange cards on Valentine's Day. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Presidents' Day n. /PREZ-ih-dents DAY/ Presidents' Day honors past leaders. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Easter n. /EES-ter/ Children hunt for Easter eggs in the park. Fingerspell EASTER
5 Mother's Day n. /MUTH-erz DAY/ Celebrate Mother's Day with flowers. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Father's Day n. /FAH-therz DAY/ Father's Day is in June. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Independence Day n. /in-dih-PEN-dents DAY/ Fireworks on Independence Day light up the sky. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Labor Day n. /LAY-ber DAY/ Labor Day honors all workers. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Halloween n. /HAL-oh-ween/ Dress in costume on Halloween night. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Veterans Day n. /VET-er-unz DAY/ Veterans Day honors those who served. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Thanksgiving n. /THANKS-giv-ing/ The family gathers for Thanksgiving dinner. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Christmas n. /KRIS-mus/ Decorate the tree for Christmas. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Hanukkah n. /HAH-nuh-kuh/ Light the menorah during Hanukkah. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Kwanzaa n. /KWAN-zuh/ Kwanzaa celebrates African heritage. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Ramadan n. /RAH-muh-don/ Ramadan is a month of fasting. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Eid n. /EED/ Eid marks the end of Ramadan. Fingerspell EID
17 Diwali n. /dih-WAH-lee/ Diwali is the festival of lights. Fingerspell DIWALI
18 Lunar New Year n. /LOO-ner NOO YEER/ Lunar New Year celebrations last many days. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Birthday n. /BURTH-day/ Blow out the candles on your birthday cake. Middle finger touches chin, then chest
20 Anniversary n. /an-ih-VUR-ser-ee/ An anniversary marks a special date each year. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Memorial Day n. /meh-MOR-ee-ul DAY/ Memorial Day remembers fallen soldiers. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Martin Luther King Day n. /MAR-tin LOO-ther KING DAY/ MLK Day celebrates equality. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Earth Day n. /URTH DAY/ Plant a tree on Earth Day, April twenty-second. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Graduation day n. /graj-oo-AY-shun DAY/ Graduation day is a proud milestone. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Back to school n. /BAK too SKOOL/ Back to school means new supplies and new friends. Fingerspell or sign each word
