Scene 19: Voting & Elections

At the Sophia Civic Center, Dara exercises the power of the vote

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Vote n. /VOHT/ Every citizen has the right to vote. F-hand drops into opposite O-hand (putting ballot in box)
2 Ballot n. /BAL-ut/ Mark your choices on the ballot. Fingerspell BALLOT
3 Candidate n. /KAN-dih-dayt/ The candidate gave a speech at the rally. C-hand shakes in space or fingerspell
4 Election n. /ee-LEK-shun/ The election is held on the first Tuesday of November. E-hand + VOTE motion
5 Register n. /REJ-ih-ster/ Register to vote before the deadline. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Polling place n. /POHL-ing PLAYS/ Go to your polling place to cast your vote. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Campaign n. /kam-PAYN/ The campaign lasted several months. C-hand pumps forward repeatedly
8 Debate n. /dih-BAYT/ The candidates debated on television. Index fingers point at each other alternately
9 Platform n. /PLAT-form/ The party platform outlines their goals. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Democrat n. /DEM-oh-krat/ The Democrat ran for city council. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Republican n. /rih-PUB-lih-kun/ The Republican candidate won the local race. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Independent n. /in-dih-PEN-dent/ An independent candidate has no party. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Issue n. /ISH-oo/ Education is an important issue this election. Fingerspell ISSUE
14 Polling n. /POHL-ing/ Polling shows the race is very close. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Absentee ballot n. /AB-sen-tee BAL-ut/ An absentee ballot lets you vote by mail. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Primary n. /PRY-mair-ee/ The primary election narrows the candidates. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 General election n. /JEN-er-ul ee-LEK-shun/ The general election determines the winner. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Inauguration n. /in-aw-gyoo-RAY-shun/ The inauguration ceremony is in January. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Governor n. /GUV-er-ner/ The governor leads the state government. G-hand circles at temple
20 Mayor n. /MAY-er/ The mayor is the leader of the city. M-hand at shoulder, like official
21 Senator n. /SEN-uh-ter/ Each state elects two senators. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Representative n. /rep-rih-ZEN-tuh-tiv/ Your representative speaks for your district. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Amendment n. /uh-MEND-ment/ An amendment changes the constitution. A-hand rewrites on opposite palm
24 Democracy n. /dih-MOK-ruh-see/ Democracy means government by the people. D-hands circle each other
25 Civic duty n. /SIV-ik DOO-tee/ Voting is a civic duty. Fingerspell or sign each word
