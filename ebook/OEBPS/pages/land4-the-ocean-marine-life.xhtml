Scene 1: The Ocean & Marine Life

Ronan and Nerissa dive beneath the waves of Aquaria's Great Reef. Every creature tells a story of the sea.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Ocean n. /OH-shun/ The ocean covers more than seventy percent of the Earth. Both hands make waves, push forward
2 Sea n. /SEE/ The sea is calm and blue near the shore. Fingerspell SEA
3 Wave v. /WAYV/ Big waves crash against the rocky cliff. Open hand waves side to side
4 Tide n. /TYD/ The tide comes in and goes out twice each day. Fingerspell TIDE
5 Current n. /KUR-ent/ A strong current carries water across the ocean. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Coral n. /KOR-ul/ Colorful coral grows in warm, shallow water. Fingerspell CORAL
7 Coral reef n. /KOR-ul REEF/ The coral reef is home to thousands of fish. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Fish n. /FISH/ Tropical fish have bright stripes and spots. Flat hand swims forward like fish
9 Shark n. /SHARK/ The shark glides through the deep, dark water. Fingerspell SHARK
10 Dolphin n. /DOL-fin/ The friendly dolphin leaps beside the boat. B-hand dips and rises forward
11 Whale n. /WAYL/ The blue whale is the largest animal on Earth. W-hand dips and rises (swimming)
12 Octopus n. /OK-tuh-pus/ The octopus has eight long arms. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Jellyfish n. /JEL-ee-fish/ The transparent jellyfish floats gently in the current. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Sea turtle n. /SEE TUR-tul/ The sea turtle swims thousands of miles to lay eggs. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Seahorse n. /SEE-hors/ The tiny seahorse wraps its tail around the seaweed. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Starfish n. /STAR-fish/ The starfish has five arms and moves slowly on the rocks. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Crab n. /KRAB/ The crab walks sideways along the sandy bottom. Both V-hands snap open and close (pincers)
18 Lobster n. /LOB-ster/ The lobster hides among the rocks at night. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Shrimp n. /SHRIMP/ Tiny shrimp swim in groups near the reef. Fingerspell SHRIMP
20 Clam n. /KLAM/ The clam opens and closes its shell for food. Fingerspell CLAM
21 Oyster n. /OY-ster/ An oyster can make a pearl inside its shell. Fingerspell OYSTER
22 Seaweed n. /SEE-weed/ Long strands of seaweed sway in the tide. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Sand n. /SAND/ The sand on the ocean floor is white and soft. Fingerspell SAND
24 Shell n. /SHEL/ Nerissa collects a beautiful shell from the beach. Fingerspell SHELL
25 Anchor n. /ANG-ker/ The anchor keeps the boat from drifting away. Fingerspell ANCHOR
26 Submarine n. /SUB-muh-reen/ A submarine can travel deep under the sea. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 Scuba diver n. /SKOO-buh DY-ver/ The scuba diver explores the reef up close. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Snorkel n. /SNOR-kul/ Ronan breathes through a snorkel near the surface. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Wetsuit n. /WET-soot/ The wetsuit keeps you warm in cold ocean water. Fingerspell or sign each word
30 Plankton n. /PLANGK-tun/ Tiny plankton is food for whales and small fish. Fingerspell or sign each word
31 Trench n. /TRENCH/ The ocean trench is the deepest part of the sea. Fingerspell TRENCH
32 Shipwreck n. /SHIP-rek/ An old shipwreck lies on the ocean floor. Fingerspell or sign each word
33 Reef n. /REEF/ The reef is alive with color and movement. Fingerspell REEF
34 Saltwater n. /SAWLT-waw-ter/ The ocean is made of saltwater. Fingerspell or sign each word
35 Marine n. /muh-REEN/ Marine life includes all plants and animals in the sea. Fingerspell MARINE
