Scene 17: Travel Documents

At the Aquaria Border Office, papers open doors

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Passport n. /PAS-port/ You need a passport to travel to other countries. P-hand opens like small book
2 Visa n. /VEE-zuh/ Apply for a visa before your trip. V-hand stamps opposite palm
3 ID n. /EYE-DEE/ Show your ID at the airport security. Fingerspell ID
4 Birth certificate n. /BURTH ser-TIF-ih-kit/ A birth certificate proves where you were born. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Social Security n. /SOH-shul seh-KYOOR-ih-tee/ Your Social Security number is private. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Green card n. /GREEN KARD/ A green card allows permanent residence. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Citizenship n. /SIT-ih-zen-ship/ She studied hard to earn her citizenship. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Application n. /ap-lih-KAY-shun/ Fill out the application completely. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Form n. /FORM/ Sign the form at the bottom of the page. Fingerspell FORM
10 Photo n. /FOH-toh/ Bring two passport photos for the application. Fingerspell PHOTO
11 Signature n. /SIG-nuh-chur/ Write your signature on the dotted line. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Expiration n. /ek-spih-RAY-shun/ Check the expiration date on your passport. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Renewal n. /rih-NOO-ul/ Submit a renewal before your visa expires. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Embassy n. /EM-buh-see/ Visit the embassy for help abroad. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Consulate n. /KON-suh-lut/ The consulate can replace a lost passport. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Customs n. /KUS-tumz/ Declare your items at customs. C-hand stamps opposite palm
17 Immigration n. /im-ih-GRAY-shun/ The immigration officer checks your passport. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Boarding pass n. /BOR-ding PAS/ Show your boarding pass to enter the plane. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Luggage n. /LUG-ij/ Check your luggage at the counter. Mime carrying suitcase (hand grips, arm curls)
20 Declaration n. /dek-luh-RAY-shun/ Fill out the customs declaration form. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Stamp n. /STAMP/ The officer stamps your passport. Fingerspell STAMP
22 Arrival n. /uh-RY-vul/ Your arrival time is three PM. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Departure n. /dih-PAR-chur/ The departure gate is B twelve. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 International n. /in-ter-NASH-un-ul/ The international terminal has flights worldwide. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Domestic n. /doh-MES-tik/ Domestic flights stay within the country. Fingerspell or sign each word
