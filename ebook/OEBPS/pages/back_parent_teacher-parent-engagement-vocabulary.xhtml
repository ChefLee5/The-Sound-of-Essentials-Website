Scene 2: Parent Engagement Vocabulary

These words help parents communicate with schools and support their children's learning.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Principal n. /PRIN-suh-pul/ The principal is the leader of the school. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Counselor n. /KOWN-suh-ler/ The school counselor helps students with problems. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 PTA n. /PEE-TEE-AY/ Join the PTA to be involved in your child's school. Fingerspell PTA
4 Volunteer n. /vol-un-TEER/ Parents can volunteer in their child's classroom. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Field trip n. /FEELD TRIP/ The class is going on a field trip to the museum. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Lunch menu n. /LUNCH MEN-yoo/ Check the lunch menu for the week online. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Backpack n. /BAK-pak/ Pack your backpack with books and supplies. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Supply list n. /suh-PLY LIST/ Buy everything on the school supply list. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Uniform n. /YOO-nih-form/ Students must wear the school uniform every day. U-hands brush down chest
10 Pick up n. /PIK UP/ Pick up your child from school at three fifteen. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Drop off n. /DROP OFF/ Drop off your child at the front entrance. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Car line n. /KAR LYN/ Wait in the car line during morning drop-off. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Bus route n. /BUS ROOT/ Find your child's bus route number on the website. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 After-school n. /AF-ter SKOOL/ The after-school program runs until five thirty. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Progress report n. /PROG-res rih-PORT/ The progress report shows how your child is doing. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Reading level n. /REE-ding LEV-ul/ Ask the teacher about your child's reading level. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Behavior n. /bih-HAYV-yer/ Good behavior in class is expected of all students. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Discipline n. /DIS-ih-plin/ The school has a fair discipline policy. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Special needs n. /SPESH-ul NEEDZ/ Students with special needs receive extra support. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Bilingual n. /by-LING-gwul/ The school offers a bilingual education program. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Interpreter n. /in-TUR-prih-ter/ Request an interpreter if you need language support. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Scholarship n. /SKOL-er-ship/ Apply for a scholarship to help pay for college. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Diploma n. /dih-PLOH-muh/ A high school diploma is required for many jobs. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Transcript n. /TRAN-skript/ Request your transcript for college applications. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Milestone n. /MYL-stohn/ Your child's first day of school is a big milestone. Fingerspell or sign each word
