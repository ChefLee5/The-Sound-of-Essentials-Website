Scene 8: Community Places

Ronan and Nerissa explore a new town in Aquaria, finding every important place a community needs.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 School n. /SKOOL/ Every neighborhood has a school for children. Clap hands twice
2 Library n. /LY-brair-ee/ The public library lends books for free. L-hand circles in air
3 Hospital n. /HOS-pih-tul/ Go to the hospital for emergency medical care. H-hand draws cross on upper arm
4 Fire station n. /FYR STAY-shun/ The fire station has trucks and brave firefighters. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Police station n. /puh-LEES STAY-shun/ Report a crime at the police station. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Post office n. /POHST OF-is/ Mail a letter or package at the post office. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Bank n. /BANGK/ The bank opens at nine in the morning. B-hand taps opposite palm repeatedly (stacking money)
8 Grocery store n. /GROH-ser-ee STOR/ Buy food and household items at the grocery store. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Restaurant n. /RES-tuh-rawnt/ The restaurant serves lunch from eleven to two. R-hand touches each side of chin
10 Pharmacy n. /FAR-muh-see/ The pharmacy fills prescriptions and sells medicine. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Gas station n. /GAS STAY-shun/ Buy fuel for your car at the gas station. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Laundromat n. /LAWN-droh-mat/ Wash your clothes at the laundromat. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Park n. /PARK/ Children play on the swings in the park. Modified P-hand taps S-hand on top
14 Playground n. /PLAY-grownd/ The playground has slides, swings, and monkey bars. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Community center n. /kuh-MYOO-nih-tee SEN-ter/ The community center hosts classes and events. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Place of worship n. /PLAYS uv WUR-ship/ A place of worship is where people gather for prayer. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 City hall n. /SIT-ee HAWL/ City hall is where the mayor works. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Courthouse n. /KORT-hows/ Legal cases are heard in the courthouse. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Museum n. /myoo-ZEE-um/ The museum has exhibits about history and art. M-hands draw building outline
20 Movie theater n. /MOO-vee THEE-uh-ter/ Watch a film at the movie theater. Fingerspell or sign each word
