Scene 1: Occupations & Careers

Ezra and Athena visit the Sophia Career Fair, where workers from every field share what they do.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Job n. /JOB/ A job is work you do to earn money. S-hand taps opposite wrist (WORK)
2 Career n. /kuh-REER/ A career is a long-term path in a field you love. Fingerspell CAREER
3 Boss n. /BOS/ The boss manages the workers in the office. B-hand taps shoulder (military stripes)
4 Employee n. /em-PLOY-ee/ An employee works for a company or business. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Employer n. /em-PLOY-er/ An employer is the person or company that hires you. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Salary n. /SAL-uh-ree/ A salary is the money paid for a full year of work. Flat hand scoops off opposite palm toward body
7 Wages n. /WAY-jez/ Wages are money paid by the hour. Fingerspell WAGES
8 Benefits n. /BEN-ih-fits/ Benefits may include health insurance and vacation days. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Resume n. /REZ-oo-may/ A resume lists your skills, education, and work experience. Fingerspell RESUME
10 Interview n. /IN-ter-vyoo/ Dress professionally for the job interview. I-hands alternate near mouth
11 Application n. /ap-lih-KAY-shun/ Fill out the application form to apply for the job. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Hire n. /HYR/ The company will hire two new employees next month. H-hand sweeps in toward body
13 Promotion n. /proh-MOH-shun/ A promotion means moving to a higher position. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Part-time n. /PART-tym/ A part-time job is fewer than forty hours a week. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Full-time n. /FOOL-tym/ A full-time job is usually forty hours per week. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Shift n. /SHIFT/ Ezra works the morning shift from six to two. Fingerspell SHIFT
17 Overtime n. /OH-ver-tym/ Working extra hours is called overtime. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Coworker n. /KOH-wur-ker/ A coworker is a person you work with. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Manager n. /MAN-uh-jer/ The manager assigns tasks to the team. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Retire n. /rih-TYR/ Grandfather plans to retire at age sixty-five. Both R-hands relax downward
21 Volunteer n. /vol-un-TEER/ A volunteer works without pay to help the community. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Skill n. /SKIL/ Typing is a useful skill for office work. Fingerspell SKILL
23 Experience n. /ek-SPEER-ee-ens/ Three years of experience is required for this position. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Qualification n. /kwol-ih-fih-KAY-shun/ A qualification proves you are trained for the job. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Deadline n. /DED-lyn/ The deadline for the project is Friday. Fingerspell or sign each word
