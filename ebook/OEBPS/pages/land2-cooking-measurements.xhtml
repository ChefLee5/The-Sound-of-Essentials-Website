Scene 14: Cooking Measurements

In the Numeria Kitchen Lab, Kwame and Octavia bake a cake while learning to measure ingredients precisely.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Recipe n. /RES-ih-pee/ Follow the recipe step by step. Fingerspell RECIPE
2 Ingredient n. /in-GREE-dee-ent/ Flour is an important ingredient in bread. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Measure v. /MEZH-er/ Measure the sugar carefully before adding it. Both Y-hands tap together (SAME SIZE)
4 Cup n. /KUP/ Add one cup of milk to the batter. C-hand taps opposite flat palm (cup on saucer)
5 Tablespoon n. /TAY-bul-spoon/ Use two tablespoons of butter. Fingerspell T-B-S or sign BIG + SPOON
6 Teaspoon n. /TEE-spoon/ Add half a teaspoon of vanilla extract. Fingerspell T-S-P or sign SMALL + SPOON
7 Pinch n. /PINCH/ A pinch of salt is a tiny amount between two fingers. Fingerspell PINCH
8 Dash n. /DASH/ A dash of pepper adds flavor to the soup. Fingerspell DASH
9 Stir v. /STUR/ Stir the batter until it is smooth. Fingerspell STIR
10 Mix v. /MIKS/ Mix the dry ingredients together first. Fingerspell MIX
11 Pour v. /POR/ Pour the batter into the baking pan. Fingerspell POUR
12 Preheat n. /pree-HEET/ Preheat the oven to 350 degrees. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Bake v. /BAYK/ Bake the cookies for twelve minutes. Fingerspell BAKE
14 Cool adj. /KOOL/ Let the cake cool before adding frosting. Both open hands fan at body
15 Serving n. /SUR-ving/ One serving of rice is one half cup. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Serving size n. /SUR-ving SYZ/ The serving size is listed on the label. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Yield n. /YEELD/ This recipe yields twenty-four cookies. Fingerspell YIELD
18 Double n. /DUB-ul/ Double the recipe to make enough for everyone. Fingerspell DOUBLE
19 Halve n. /HAV/ Halve the recipe if you only need a small batch. Fingerspell HALVE
20 Level n. /LEV-ul/ Use a level cup of flour, not a heaping one. Fingerspell LEVEL
21 Heaping n. /HEEP-ing/ A heaping tablespoon is piled above the rim. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Sift n. /SIFT/ Sift the flour to remove lumps. Fingerspell SIFT
23 Whisk n. /WISK/ Whisk the eggs until they are fluffy. Fingerspell WHISK
24 Grease n. /GREES/ Grease the pan so the cake doesn't stick. Fingerspell GREASE
25 Batch n. /BACH/ The first batch of cookies is ready! Fingerspell BATCH
26 Timer n. /TY-mer/ Set the timer so the bread doesn't burn. T-hand taps wrist
27 Degrees n. /dih-GREEZ/ Bake at three hundred fifty degrees Fahrenheit. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Measuring cup n. /MEZH-er-ing KUP/ Use a measuring cup for liquid ingredients. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Measuring spoon n. /MEZH-er-ing SPOON/ The measuring spoon set has four sizes. Fingerspell or sign each word
30 Spatula n. /SPACH-uh-luh/ Use a spatula to scrape the bowl clean. Fingerspell or sign each word
🧁

Octavia's Tip:

"Cooking IS math! When you MEASURE, you use fractions. When you DOUBLE a recipe, you multiply. When you HALVE it, you divide. Bake a cake and do math at the same time!"
