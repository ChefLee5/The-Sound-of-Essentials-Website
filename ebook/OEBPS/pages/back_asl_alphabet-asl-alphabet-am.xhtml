Scene 1: ASL Alphabet A–M

Every letter has a handshape. Learn the ASL Manual Alphabet — your bridge to fingerspelling any English word!

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 A n. /AY/ The letter A starts the alphabet. Fist with thumb at side
2 B n. /BEE/ B is for Book, Bus, and Banana. Four fingers up, thumb across palm
3 C n. /SEE/ C is for Cat, City, and Cup. Curved hand like the letter C
4 D n. /DEE/ D is for Dog, Day, and Door. Index up, other fingers touch thumb
5 E n. /EE/ E is for Eat, Eye, and Energy. Fingertips curled to thumb, thumb tucked
6 F n. /EF/ F is for Family, Fish, and Flower. Three fingers up, index and thumb circle
7 G n. /JEE/ G is for Good, Green, and Garden. Index and thumb point sideways
8 H n. /AYCH/ H is for Happy, Home, and Help. Index and middle point sideways, stacked
9 I n. /EYE/ I is for I, Ice, and Important. Pinky finger up, rest closed
10 J n. /JAY/ J is for Juice, Job, and January. Pinky up, trace letter J in air
11 K n. /KAY/ K is for Kitchen, Key, and Kind. Index and middle up, thumb between
12 L n. /EL/ L is for Love, Lake, and Library. L-shape: index up, thumb out
13 M n. /EM/ M is for Mother, Map, and Music. Three fingers over thumb, fingers down
