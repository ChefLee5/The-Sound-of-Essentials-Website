Scene 12: Mail & Postal Services

In Aquaria, letters and packages connect people across oceans. Ronan and Nerissa visit the post office.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Letter n. /LET-er/ I wrote a letter to my grandmother. Fingerspell LETTER
2 Envelope n. /EN-vuh-lohp/ Put the letter inside the envelope. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Stamp n. /STAMP/ Buy a stamp to mail the letter. Fingerspell STAMP
4 Address n. /AD-res/ Write the address on the front of the envelope. A-hands move up chest (like suspenders)
5 Return address n. /rih-TURN AD-res/ The return address is in the top left corner. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Zip code n. /ZIP KOHD/ The zip code helps mail get to the right place. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Package n. /PAK-ij/ The package arrived in a brown cardboard box. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Mail carrier n. /MAYL KAIR-ee-er/ The mail carrier delivers letters to every house. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Post office n. /POHST OF-is/ Go to the post office to send a package. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Mailbox n. /MAYL-boks/ Drop the letter in the blue mailbox. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Postcard n. /POHST-kard/ Nerissa sends a postcard from every city she visits. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Delivery n. /dih-LIV-er-ee/ The delivery truck brings packages to the door. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Tracking number n. /TRAK-ing NUM-ber/ Use the tracking number to find your package online. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Fragile n. /FRAJ-ul/ Mark the box "Fragile" if it contains glass. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Priority mail n. /pry-OR-ih-tee MAYL/ Priority mail arrives faster than regular mail. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Express n. /ek-SPRES/ Express delivery gets the package there by tomorrow. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Scale n. /SKAYL/ The clerk weighs the package on the scale. Both flat hands balance like scale
18 Certified mail n. /SUR-tih-fyd MAYL/ Certified mail requires a signature when delivered. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 P.O. Box n. /PEE-OH BOKS/ Some people receive mail at a P.O. Box. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Sort n. /SORT/ Workers sort the mail by zip code. Fingerspell SORT

Nerissa's Tip:
