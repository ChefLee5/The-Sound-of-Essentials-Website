Scene 6: Healthy Habits & Hygiene

Every morning, Felix and Amara follow their Vitalis Wellness Routine. Clean body, clear mind!

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Hygiene n. /HY-jeen/ Good hygiene keeps your body clean and healthy. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Wash v. /WOSH/ Wash your hands with soap for twenty seconds. Fists rub together (scrubbing)
3 Soap n. /SOHP/ Soap kills germs on your skin. Bend fingers on opposite palm
4 Shampoo n. /sham-POO/ Amara works the shampoo into her hair and rinses it out. Both claw hands scrub on head
5 Conditioner n. /kun-DISH-un-er/ Conditioner makes hair soft and easy to comb. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Shower n. /SHOW-er/ Felix takes a warm shower every morning. 5-hand above head opens/closes (water spraying)
7 Bath n. /BATH/ A long bath with warm water relaxes tired muscles. Both A-fists scrub up and down chest
8 Towel n. /TOW-ul/ Dry off with a clean towel after every shower. Flat hands rub back and forth at sides
9 Deodorant n. /dee-OH-der-unt/ Deodorant keeps you smelling fresh all day. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Toothbrush n. /TOOTH-brush/ Brush your teeth for two full minutes. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Toothpaste n. /TOOTH-payst/ Squeeze a pea-sized amount of toothpaste on the brush. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Floss n. /FLOS/ Floss reaches places the toothbrush cannot. Fingerspell FLOSS
13 Comb v. /KOHM/ Amara runs a wide-tooth comb through her hair. Fingerspell COMB
14 Brush v. /BRUSH/ A soft brush detangles hair without pulling. Mime brushing teeth
15 Nail clipper n. /NAYL KLIP-er/ Use a nail clipper to trim your fingernails and toenails. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Sunscreen n. /SUN-skreen/ Apply sunscreen before going outside in the sun. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Lotion n. /LOH-shun/ Lotion keeps skin soft and prevents dryness. Fingerspell LOTION
18 Tissue n. /TISH-oo/ Cover your nose with a tissue when you sneeze. Fingerspell TISSUE
19 Hand sanitizer n. /HAND SAN-ih-ty-zer/ Use hand sanitizer when soap and water aren't available. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Sleep v. /SLEEP/ Eight to ten hours of sleep helps children grow strong. Open hand draws down face, eyes close
21 Rest v. /REST/ Your body needs rest after a long day of activity. Arms cross over chest (resting)
22 Stretch v. /STRECH/ Stretch your arms and legs when you wake up. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Posture n. /POS-chur/ Good posture means sitting and standing up straight. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Hydrate n. /HY-drayt/ Drink water throughout the day to stay hydrated. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Germs n. /JURMZ/ Germs are tiny organisms that can make you sick. Fingerspell GERMS
26 Immune system n. /ih-MYOON SIS-tem/ The immune system protects the body from illness. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 Wellness n. /WEL-nes/ Wellness means taking care of your body AND your mind. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Routine n. /roo-TEEN/ A daily routine helps you build healthy habits. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Laundry n. /LAWN-dree/ Put dirty clothes in the laundry basket to be washed. Fingerspell or sign each word
30 Clean v. /KLEEN/ A clean home keeps everyone in the family healthy. Flat hand slides across opposite palm
🧼

Amara's Tip:

"Let's practice a routine! Say: 'First, I wake up. Then, I brush my teeth. Next, I wash my face. After that, I eat breakfast.' When you say it in order, it becomes easy to remember!"
