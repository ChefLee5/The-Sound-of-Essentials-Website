Scene 12: Ages & Life Stages

The Garden of Time grows a tree for every stage of life. Elias and Selene walk along its rings.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Baby n. /BAY-bee/ A baby is a child from birth to about one year old. Rock arms like cradling baby
2 Toddler n. /TOD-ler/ A toddler is learning to walk and talk. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Child n. /CHYLD/ A child is a young person between two and twelve. Pat downward in air (showing height)
4 Teenager n. /TEEN-ay-jer/ A teenager is between thirteen and nineteen years old. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Adult n. /uh-DULT/ An adult is a fully grown person, usually eighteen or older. A-hand moves from forehead to chest
6 Senior n. /SEEN-yer/ A senior citizen is over sixty-five years old. Fingerspell SENIOR
7 Age n. /AYJ/ What is your age? I am thirty-two years old. Pull chin down with C-hand
8 Birthday n. /BURTH-day/ Your birthday is the day you were born. Middle finger touches chin, then chest
9 Anniversary n. /an-ih-VUR-suh-ree/ An anniversary marks years since a special event. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Generation n. /jen-er-AY-shun/ A generation is about twenty-five to thirty years. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Youth n. /YOOTH/ Youth is the time of life when you are young. Fingerspell YOUTH
12 Elderly adv. /EL-der-lee/ The elderly deserve our respect and care. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Grow up n. /GROH UP/ What do you want to be when you grow up? Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Milestone n. /MYL-stohn/ A first word is a milestone in every baby's life. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Lifespan n. /LYF-span/ The average human lifespan is about seventy-five years. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Mature n. /muh-CHOOR/ To mature means to grow older and wiser. Fingerspell MATURE
17 Infant n. /IN-funt/ An infant needs constant care from a parent. Fingerspell INFANT
18 Middle-aged n. /MID-ul AYJD/ A middle-aged person is usually between forty and sixty. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Aging n. /AY-jing/ Aging is a natural part of every living thing. Fingerspell AGING
20 Memory n. /MEM-or-ee/ A memory is something you remember from the past. Fingerspell MEMORY
