Scene 17: School Schedules & Timetables

Selene maps out every period, break, and deadline

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Period n. /PEER-ee-ud/ First period starts at eight o'clock. Fingerspell PERIOD
2 Bell n. /BEL/ The bell rings to start and end each class. Fingerspell BELL
3 Homeroom n. /HOHM-room/ Check in with your teacher during homeroom. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Advisory n. /ad-VY-zor-ee/ Advisory is a short class for announcements. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Break n. /BRAYK/ We have a ten-minute break between classes. Fingerspell BREAK
6 Lunch period n. /LUNCH PEER-ee-ud/ Lunch period is from noon to twelve thirty. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 After school n. /AF-ter SKOOL/ Stay for after school tutoring. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Early dismissal n. /UR-lee dis-MIS-ul/ Early dismissal is at one o'clock today. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Late start n. /LAYT START/ Late start means school begins at ten. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Snow day n. /SNOH DAY/ A snow day means no school due to weather. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Substitute n. /SUB-stih-toot/ A substitute teacher fills in today. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Syllabus n. /SIL-uh-bus/ The syllabus outlines the course. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Due date n. /DOO DAYT/ The project due date is Friday. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Midterm n. /MID-turm/ Study hard for the midterm exam. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Final n. /FY-nul/ The final exam covers the entire semester. Fingerspell FINAL
16 Quarter n. /KWOR-ter/ Report cards are issued every quarter. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Trimester n. /try-MES-ter/ The school year has three trimesters. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 School year n. /SKOOL YEER/ The school year runs from August to June. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Summer break n. /SUM-er BRAYK/ Summer break lasts about ten weeks. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Spring break n. /SPRING BRAYK/ Spring break is one week in March or April. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Winter break n. /WIN-ter BRAYK/ Winter break includes two weeks off. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Tardy n. /TAR-dee/ Three tardies equal one absence. Fingerspell TARDY
23 Absent n. /AB-sent/ Mark the student absent if they are not here. Fingerspell ABSENT
24 Present n. /PREZ-ent/ Raise your hand to be marked present. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Attendance n. /uh-TEN-dents/ The teacher takes attendance every morning. Fingerspell or sign each word
