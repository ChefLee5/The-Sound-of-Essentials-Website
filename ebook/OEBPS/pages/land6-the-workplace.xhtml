Scene 3: The Workplace

Ezra shows the inside of a typical Sophia office. Everything has a name and a function.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Office n. /OF-is/ The office has desks, computers, and a copier. O-hands outline box shape
2 Desk n. /DESK/ Ezra keeps his files organized on his desk. Sign TABLE (forearms stacked, then flat)
3 Computer n. /kum-PYOO-ter/ The computer is the main tool for most office work. C-hand bounces up opposite forearm
4 Printer n. /PRIN-ter/ The printer makes paper copies of digital documents. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Copier n. /KOP-ee-er/ Use the copier to make duplicates of the report. Fingerspell COPIER
6 File cabinet n. /FYL KAB-ih-net/ Old documents are stored in the file cabinet. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Stapler n. /STAY-pler/ Use the stapler to hold pages together. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Paper clip n. /PAY-per KLIP/ A paper clip holds a few pages together. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Sticky notes n. /STIK-ee NOHTS/ Write reminders on sticky notes and post them on your monitor. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Meeting n. /MEE-ting/ The team meeting is at ten o'clock in the conference room. Both 5-hands come together, fingertips meet
11 Conference room n. /KON-fer-ens ROOM/ The conference room has a long table and a projector. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Break room n. /BRAYK ROOM/ Eat lunch and relax in the break room. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Cubicle n. /KYOO-bih-kul/ Each worker has a cubicle with walls for privacy. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Supervisor n. /SOO-per-vy-zer/ The supervisor checks in with the team each morning. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Project n. /PROJ-ekt/ The team is working on a big project this month. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Report n. /rih-PORT/ Write a report at the end of every week. Fingerspell REPORT
17 Email n. /EE-mayl/ Send the document by email. Index shoots into opposite C-hand (mail + computer)
18 Schedule n. /SKED-yool/ Check the schedule for today's meetings. 4-hand slides down, then across opposite palm
19 Paycheck n. /PAY-chek/ Ezra receives his paycheck every two weeks. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Time card n. /TYM KARD/ Swipe your time card to clock in and out. Fingerspell or sign each word
🏢

Ezra's Tip:

"Even if you've never been in an office, knowing these words prepares you. Practice: 'I work at a DESK. I use a COMPUTER. I go to MEETINGS.' Every word brings you closer to your goals."
