Scene 19: The Neighborhood

Kenji and Aiko explore their neighborhood, discovering all the places and people nearby.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Neighbor n. /NAY-ber/ A neighbor is someone who lives next to you. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Block n. /BLOK/ Walk around the block for exercise. Fingerspell BLOCK
3 Corner n. /KOR-ner/ The bakery is on the corner of Oak Street. Fingerspell CORNER
4 Lane n. /LAYN/ Our house is on a quiet lane with big trees. Fingerspell LANE
5 Avenue n. /AV-eh-noo/ The parade goes down the main avenue. Fingerspell AVENUE
6 Apartment n. /uh-PART-ment/ We live in an apartment on the third floor. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Townhouse n. /TOWN-hows/ A townhouse shares walls with its neighbors. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Yard n. /YARD/ The children play in the front yard. Fingerspell YARD
9 Community n. /kuh-MYOO-nih-tee/ Our community has a park, library, and school. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Park n. /PARK/ The park has swings, slides, and a basketball court. Modified P-hand taps S-hand on top
11 Library n. /LY-brair-ee/ Borrow books for free at the public library. L-hand circles in air
12 Church n. /CHURCH/ The church opens its doors for community meals. C-hand taps back of S-fist (building on rock)
13 Mosque n. /MOSK/ The mosque is a place of worship and community. Fingerspell MOSQUE
14 Temple n. /TEM-pul/ The temple hosts cultural celebrations. Fingerspell TEMPLE
15 Grocery store n. /GROH-ser-ee STOR/ The grocery store is three blocks from our house. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Laundromat n. /LAWN-droh-mat/ Wash your clothes at the laundromat. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Pharmacy n. /FAR-muh-see/ The pharmacy is next to the doctor's office. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Gas station n. /GAS STAY-shun/ Fill up the tank at the gas station. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Barbershop n. /BAR-ber-shop/ Get a haircut at the barbershop on Main Street. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Salon n. /suh-LON/ She gets her hair done at the salon. Fingerspell SALON
21 Daycare n. /DAY-kair/ Drop off the baby at daycare before work. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Clinic n. /KLIN-ik/ The community clinic provides low-cost health care. Fingerspell CLINIC
23 Senior center n. /SEEN-yer SEN-ter/ The senior center offers activities for older adults. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Playground n. /PLAY-grownd/ Meet me at the playground after school. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Community garden n. /kuh-MYOO-nih-tee GAR-den/ Grow vegetables in the community garden. Fingerspell or sign each word

Aiko's Tip:

"Take a 'neighborhood vocabulary walk!' Name every place you pass: PHARMACY, LIBRARY, PARK, LAUNDROMAT. If you can name 20 places, you already know your neighborhood in English!"
