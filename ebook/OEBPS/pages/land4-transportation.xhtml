Scene 3: Transportation

Ronan and Nerissa travel across Aquaria using every kind of vehicle — by land, sea, and air.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Car n. /KAR/ The car drives along the highway. Both C-hands mime steering wheel
2 Truck n. /TRUK/ The big truck carries goods across the country. Fingerspell TRUCK
3 Bus n. /BUS/ Take the bus to school and back home. B-hand mimes long vehicle
4 Van n. /VAN/ The family loads luggage into the van. Fingerspell VAN
5 Taxi n. /TAK-see/ Hail a taxi to get to the airport quickly. Fingerspell T-A-X-I or sign CAR + YELLOW
6 Motorcycle n. /MOH-ter-sy-kul/ The motorcycle weaves through city traffic. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Bicycle n. /BY-sih-kul/ Ronan rides his bicycle along the waterfront path. S-fists pedal in circles
8 Scooter n. /SKOO-ter/ The electric scooter is quiet and fast. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Train n. /TRAYN/ The train travels on tracks from city to city. H-hands slide along each other (tracks)
10 Subway n. /SUB-way/ Take the subway to travel underground across town. Y-hand slides under opposite flat palm
11 Airplane n. /AIR-playn/ The airplane flies above the clouds. Y-hand with index out, flies forward
12 Helicopter n. /HEL-ih-kop-ter/ The helicopter can hover in one place. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Boat n. /BOHT/ Nerissa rows the small boat across the lake. Both cupped hands form hull, bounce forward
14 Ship n. /SHIP/ The large ship carries passengers across the ocean. Fingerspell SHIP
15 Ferry n. /FAIR-ee/ The ferry takes cars and people across the bay. F-hands carry across space
16 Sailboat n. /SAYL-boht/ The sailboat uses wind to move through the water. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Canoe n. /kuh-NOO/ Ronan paddles the canoe down the calm river. Fingerspell CANOE
18 Kayak n. /KY-ak/ The kayak glides through the narrow waterway. Fingerspell KAYAK
19 Ambulance n. /AM-byoo-luns/ The ambulance rushes to the emergency with sirens on. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Fire truck n. /FYR TRUK/ The red fire truck races to the building. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Police car n. /puh-LEES KAR/ The police car patrols the neighborhood. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Tow truck n. /TOH TRUK/ The tow truck takes the broken car to the repair shop. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Crosswalk n. /KROS-wawk/ Always cross the street at the crosswalk. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Traffic light n. /TRAF-ik LYT/ Stop on red, go on green at the traffic light. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Stop sign n. /STOP SYN/ Every car must stop at the stop sign. Fingerspell or sign each word
26 Speed limit n. /SPEED LIM-it/ The speed limit in a school zone is twenty miles per hour. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 Parking lot n. /PAR-king LOT/ Find a space in the parking lot near the store. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Gas station n. /GAS STAY-shun/ Fill up the car's tank at the gas station. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Highway n. /HY-way/ The highway has multiple lanes for fast driving. Both hands weave forward (busy road)
30 Intersection n. /in-ter-SEK-shun/ Two roads meet at the intersection. Fingerspell or sign each word
31 Pedestrian n. /peh-DES-tree-un/ A pedestrian is a person walking on foot. Fingerspell or sign each word
32 Sidewalk n. /SYD-wawk/ Walk on the sidewalk, not on the road. Fingerspell or sign each word
33 Bridge n. /BRIJ/ The bridge crosses over the wide river. V-hand bridges under opposite forearm
34 Tunnel n. /TUN-ul/ The tunnel goes through the mountain. Fingerspell TUNNEL
35 Route n. /ROOT/ Which route is the fastest to the airport? Fingerspell ROUTE
