Scene 15: Digital Time & Screens

Selene discovers how technology changed how we experience time

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Digital clock n. /DIJ-ih-tul KLOK/ The digital clock shows numbers instead of hands. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Timer n. /TY-mer/ Set the timer for ten minutes. T-hand taps wrist
3 Alarm n. /uh-LARM/ The alarm rings at six thirty every morning. Index taps opposite palm rapidly (ringing)
4 Countdown n. /KOWNT-down/ The countdown starts at ten, nine, eight... Fingers count down 5-4-3-2-1
5 Stopwatch n. /STOP-wotch/ Use the stopwatch to time the race. Both S-hands: one taps other (START + WATCH)
6 Snooze n. /SNOOZ/ Press snooze for five more minutes of sleep. Fingerspell SNOOZE
7 Reminder n. /rih-MYN-der/ Set a reminder for your doctor appointment. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Calendar app n. /KAL-en-der AP/ Add events to your calendar app. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Notification n. /noh-tih-fih-KAY-shun/ A notification pops up on your screen. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Screen time n. /SKREEN TYM/ Limit screen time to two hours per day. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Streaming n. /STREEM-ing/ Streaming a movie takes about two hours. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Loading n. /LOH-ding/ The page is still loading. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Buffering n. /BUF-er-ing/ The video keeps buffering because the internet is slow. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Download n. /DOWN-lohd/ Download the file in about three minutes. Claw hand pulls down to S-fist
15 Upload n. /UP-lohd/ Upload the photo to the cloud. S-fist pushes up, opens to claw
16 Update n. /up-DAYT/ The software update takes fifteen minutes. Fingerspell UPDATE
17 Real-time n. /REEL-tym/ GPS gives real-time directions. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Instant n. /IN-stunt/ The message was delivered in an instant. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Lag n. /LAG/ A lag means there is a delay in the connection. Fingerspell LAG
20 Sync n. /SINK/ Sync your watch with your phone for accuracy. Fingerspell SYNC
21 Timestamp n. /TYM-stamp/ The timestamp shows when the photo was taken. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Log in n. /LOG IN/ Log in to your account to check the schedule. Fingerspell LOG IN
23 Schedule n. /SKEJ-ool/ Schedule the meeting for tomorrow at two. 4-hand slides down, then across opposite palm
24 Time zone n. /TYM ZOHN/ Adjust for the time zone when calling overseas. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 24-hour clock n. /TWEN-tee-for OW-er KLOK/ The military uses a 24-hour clock. Fingerspell or sign each word
