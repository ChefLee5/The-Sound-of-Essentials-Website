The final set of high-frequency words completes your reading foundation.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Always n. /AWL-wayz/ Always say please and thank you. Index finger circles continuously
2 Never n. /NEV-er/ Never give up on your dreams. S-hand swoops down and away
3 Sometimes n. /SUM-tymz/ Sometimes the bus is late. Index finger bounces on opposite palm slowly
4 Usually adv. /YOO-zhoo-uh-lee/ I usually wake up at six thirty. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Often n. /OF-en/ How often do you exercise? Fingerspell OFTEN
6 Already n. /awl-RED-ee/ I already finished my homework. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Still n. /STIL/ Are you still waiting? Fingerspell STILL
8 Yet n. /YET/ I haven't eaten lunch yet. Fingerspell YET
9 Now n. /NOW/ We need to leave now. Fingerspell NOW
10 Soon n. /SOON/ The doctor will see you soon. F-hand on chin tilts forward slightly
11 Today n. /too-DAY/ Today is a beautiful day. Both Y-hands drop in front of body
12 Tomorrow n. /too-MOR-oh/ Tomorrow we have a test. Thumb of A-hand bounces forward from cheek
13 Yesterday n. /YES-ter-day/ Yesterday I went to the store. Y-hand or A-thumb touches cheek, then ear
14 Together n. /too-GETH-er/ Let's work together on this project. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Only n. /OHN-lee/ I have only five dollars left. Fingerspell ONLY
16 Even n. /EE-ven/ Even children can learn this. Fingerspell EVEN
17 Really adv. /REE-lee/ I really like your new shirt. Fingerspell REALLY
18 Too n. /TOO/ This soup is too hot. Fingerspell TOO
19 Enough n. /ee-NUF/ Do you have enough money? Fingerspell ENOUGH
20 Almost n. /AWL-mohst/ I almost missed the bus. Fingerspell ALMOST
21 Both n. /BOHTH/ Both answers are correct. Fingerspell BOTH
22 Either n. /EE-ther/ You can sit in either chair. Fingerspell EITHER
23 Neither n. /NEE-ther/ Neither answer is wrong. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Else n. /ELS/ What else do you need? Fingerspell ELSE
25 Own n. /OHN/ I want my own room someday. Fingerspell OWN
26 Such n. /SUCH/ She is such a kind person. Fingerspell SUCH
27 Much n. /MUCH/ Thank you very much! Fingerspell MUCH
28 Well n. /WEL/ She speaks English very well. Fingerspell WELL
29 Quite n. /KWYT/ The movie was quite good. Fingerspell QUITE
30 Perhaps n. /per-HAPS/ Perhaps we can try again tomorrow. Fingerspell or sign each word
