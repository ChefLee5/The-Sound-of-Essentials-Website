Scene 5: Emotions & Feelings

In the Harmonia Mood Garden, each flower represents a different emotion. Kenji and Aiko walk through and name what they feel.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Happy adj. /HAP-ee/ Kenji feels happy when he plays his instrument. Flat hand brushes up chest twice
2 Sad adj. /SAD/ Aiko feels sad when her friend moves away. Both open hands slide down face
3 Angry adj. /ANG-gree/ It's okay to feel angry, but use your words. Claw hand pulls away from face
4 Scared n. /SKAIRD/ The loud thunder made the child feel scared. Both fists open outward near chest (startle)
5 Surprised n. /ser-PRYZD/ Kenji was surprised by the birthday party. Pinch fingers near eyes, flick open
6 Excited adj. /ek-SY-ted/ Aiko is excited about the field trip tomorrow. Middle fingers brush up chest alternately
7 Nervous adj. /NUR-vus/ Kenji feels nervous before his first performance. Both 5-hands shake in front
8 Calm adj. /KAHM/ Take a deep breath and feel calm. Both flat hands push down slowly
9 Tired n. /TY-erd/ After running all day, Aiko feels tired. Bent hands on chest, fall forward
10 Bored n. /BORD/ Kenji feels bored when there is nothing to do. Index finger twists on nose
11 Confused n. /kun-FYOOZD/ The difficult question left Aiko feeling confused. Claw hands near temples circle in opposite directions
12 Proud adj. /PROWD/ Kenji is proud of the song he wrote. Thumb slides up chest
13 Frustrated n. /FRUS-tray-ted/ Aiko feels frustrated when she can't solve the puzzle. Back of hand hits chin upward
14 Shy n. /SHY/ The new student feels shy on the first day. Knuckles brush up cheek, head turns
15 Brave adj. /BRAYV/ Aiko is brave when she speaks in front of the class. Claw hands at shoulders pull to fists at chest
16 Lonely adv. /LOHN-lee/ Kenji feels lonely when his friends are away. Index finger circles in front of face
17 Grateful adj. /GRAYT-ful/ I am grateful for my family and friends. Flat hand from chin sweeps forward
18 Jealous adj. /JEL-us/ It's natural to feel jealous sometimes, but kindness is stronger. J-hand draws J from corner of mouth
19 Worried n. /WUR-eed/ Aiko is worried about the math test. W-hands circle in front of forehead
20 Hopeful adj. /HOHP-ful/ Kenji feels hopeful that tomorrow will be a good day. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Disappointed n. /dis-uh-POYNT-ed/ The cancelled trip made Aiko feel disappointed. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Embarrassed n. /em-BAIR-usd/ Kenji felt embarrassed when he tripped on stage. Both hands spread up cheeks (blushing)
23 Curious adj. /KYOOR-ee-us/ Aiko is curious about how instruments are made. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Comfortable n. /KUM-fer-tuh-bul/ The cozy room makes everyone feel comfortable. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Overwhelmed n. /oh-ver-WELMD/ Too many tasks at once can make you feel overwhelmed. Fingerspell or sign each word
26 Relaxed n. /rih-LAKSD/ After yoga, Kenji feels completely relaxed. Fingerspell or sign each word
27 Content n. /kun-TENT/ Aiko feels content sitting by the river reading. Fingerspell or sign each word
28 Anxious adj. /ANG-shus/ Kenji feels anxious before meeting new people. Fingerspell or sign each word
29 Loved n. /LUVD/ Everyone deserves to feel loved. Fingerspell LOVED
30 Safe adj. /SAYF/ Kenji feels safe when he is with his family. S-hands cross at wrists, then pull apart
