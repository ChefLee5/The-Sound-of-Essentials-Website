Scene 15: Sleep & Rest

In the Vitalis Dream Chamber, rest is medicine

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Sleep v. /SLEEP/ Get eight hours of sleep every night. Open hand draws down face, eyes close
2 Bed n. /BED/ Make your bed every morning. Fingerspell BED
3 Pillow n. /PIL-oh/ Fluff the pillow before going to sleep. Fingerspell PILLOW
4 Blanket n. /BLANK-it/ Pull the blanket up when you feel cold. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Mattress n. /MAT-res/ A good mattress supports your back. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Alarm clock n. /uh-LARM KLOK/ The alarm clock wakes you up at six. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Nap n. /NAP/ A short nap can recharge your energy. Fingerspell NAP
8 Dream n. /DREEM/ She had a wonderful dream last night. Fingerspell DREAM
9 Nightmare n. /NYT-mair/ A nightmare is a scary dream. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Rest v. /REST/ Rest your body after a long day. Arms cross over chest (resting)
11 Yawn n. /YAWN/ A yawn means you are tired. Fingerspell YAWN
12 Snore n. /SNOR/ He snores loudly when he sleeps. Fingerspell SNORE
13 Drowsy n. /DROW-zee/ Feeling drowsy means you are getting sleepy. Fingerspell DROWSY
14 Insomnia n. /in-SOM-nee-uh/ Insomnia means having trouble falling asleep. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Pajamas n. /puh-JAH-muhz/ Put on pajamas before going to bed. P-hand at cheek (SLEEP) + CLOTHES
16 Lullaby n. /LUL-uh-by/ Sing a lullaby to help the baby sleep. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Bedtime n. /BED-tym/ Bedtime is at nine o'clock on school nights. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Wake up n. /WAYK UP/ Wake up early to have a good morning. Pinch at eyes, flick open
19 Stretch v. /STRECH/ Stretch your body when you first wake up. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Rested n. /REST-ed/ I feel rested after a good night of sleep. Fingerspell RESTED
21 Toss and turn n. /TOS and TURN/ She tossed and turned all night. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Night light n. /NYT LYT/ The night light glows softly in the room. Fingerspell or sign each word
23 Sleepy n. /SLEEP-ee/ The baby looks sleepy after lunch. Fingerspell SLEEPY
24 Relax n. /rih-LAKS/ Relax your muscles before bed. Fingerspell RELAX
25 Routine n. /roo-TEEN/ A bedtime routine helps you fall asleep faster. Fingerspell or sign each word
