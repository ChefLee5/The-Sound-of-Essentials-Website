Scene 11: Nutrition & Food Groups

In the Vitalis Nutrition Lab, Felix and Amara learn about the five food groups and how to build a balanced plate.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Nutrition n. /noo-TRISH-un/ Good nutrition means eating foods that help your body. Fingerspell or sign each word
2 Protein n. /PROH-teen/ Chicken, beans, and eggs are rich in protein. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Carbohydrate n. /kar-boh-HY-drayt/ Bread and rice are carbohydrates that give you energy. Fingerspell or sign each word
4 Fiber n. /FY-ber/ Fruits, vegetables, and whole grains have lots of fiber. Fingerspell FIBER
5 Calcium n. /KAL-see-um/ Milk and cheese have calcium that makes bones strong. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Iron n. /EYE-urn/ Spinach and red meat contain iron for healthy blood. Fingerspell IRON
7 Vitamin n. /VY-tuh-min/ Oranges are full of Vitamin C. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Calorie n. /KAL-uh-ree/ A calorie is a unit that measures energy in food. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Sugar n. /SHOOG-er/ Too much sugar is bad for your teeth and body. Fingers brush down chin twice
10 Fat n. /FAT/ Healthy fats come from nuts, fish, and avocados. Fingerspell FAT
11 Dairy n. /DAIR-ee/ Milk, yogurt, and cheese belong to the dairy group. Fingerspell DAIRY
12 Grain n. /GRAYN/ Bread, oatmeal, and pasta are made from grains. Fingerspell GRAIN
13 Whole grain n. /HOHL GRAYN/ Whole grain bread is healthier than white bread. Fingerspell or sign each word
14 Serving size n. /SUR-ving SYZ/ The serving size tells you how much to eat at one time. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Balanced meal n. /BAL-unst MEEL/ A balanced meal has protein, grains, and vegetables. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Sodium n. /SOH-dee-um/ Too much sodium, or salt, raises blood pressure. Fingerspell SODIUM
17 Organic n. /or-GAN-ik/ Organic vegetables are grown without pesticides. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Portion n. /POR-shun/ A small portion of rice is about the size of your fist. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Nutrient n. /NOO-tree-unt/ Every nutrient has a job inside the body. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Food label n. /FOOD LAY-bul/ Read the food label to know what's inside the package. Fingerspell or sign each word

Felix's Tip:

"Look at your plate! Is it colorful? A plate full of different colors means different NUTRIENTS. Green spinach, red tomatoes, orange carrots — eat the rainbow!"
