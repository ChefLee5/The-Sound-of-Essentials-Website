Scene 16: Personal Care Products

At the Vitalis Hygiene Market, Zara names every product

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Shampoo n. /sham-POO/ Use shampoo to wash your hair. Both claw hands scrub on head
2 Conditioner n. /kun-DISH-un-er/ Conditioner makes hair soft and smooth. Fingerspell or sign each word
3 Soap n. /SOHP/ Wash your hands with soap and water. Bend fingers on opposite palm
4 Body wash n. /BOD-ee WOSH/ Use body wash in the shower. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Toothpaste n. /TOOTH-payst/ Squeeze toothpaste onto your toothbrush. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Toothbrush n. /TOOTH-brush/ Replace your toothbrush every three months. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Floss n. /FLOS/ Floss between your teeth every day. Fingerspell FLOSS
8 Mouthwash n. /MOWTH-wosh/ Gargle with mouthwash after brushing. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Deodorant n. /dee-OH-der-unt/ Apply deodorant under your arms. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Lotion n. /LOH-shun/ Put lotion on your hands to keep them soft. Fingerspell LOTION
11 Sunscreen n. /SUN-skreen/ Apply sunscreen before going outside. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Razor n. /RAY-zer/ Use a razor carefully to shave. Fingerspell RAZOR
13 Comb v. /KOHM/ Comb your hair to remove tangles. Fingerspell COMB
14 Brush v. /BRUSH/ Brush your hair before leaving the house. Mime brushing teeth
15 Hair dryer n. /HAIR DRY-er/ Use the hair dryer on a low setting. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Cotton ball n. /KOT-un BAWL/ Use a cotton ball to apply medicine. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 Band-Aid n. /BAND-ayd/ Put a Band-Aid on the small cut. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Tissues n. /TISH-ooz/ Grab a tissue when you need to sneeze. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Hand sanitizer n. /HAND SAN-ih-ty-zer/ Use hand sanitizer when soap is not available. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Nail clipper n. /NAYL KLIP-er/ Trim your nails with a nail clipper. Fingerspell or sign each word
21 Mirror n. /MEER-er/ Check your appearance in the mirror. Fingerspell MIRROR
22 Towel n. /TOW-ul/ Dry your face with a clean towel. Flat hands rub back and forth at sides
23 Washcloth n. /WOSH-kloth/ Scrub your face gently with a washcloth. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Tweezers n. /TWEE-zerz/ Use tweezers to remove a splinter. Fingerspell or sign each word
25 Q-tip n. /KYOO-tip/ Use a Q-tip carefully to clean your ears. Fingerspell Q-TIP
