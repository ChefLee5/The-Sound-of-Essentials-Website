Scene 11: Insects & Bugs

Under every leaf and stone, tiny creatures go about their busy lives. Vesta has a magnifying glass to study them all.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Insect n. /IN-sekt/ An insect has six legs and three body parts. Fingerspell INSECT
2 Ant n. /ANT/ The ant carries a crumb ten times its own weight. Fingerspell ANT
3 Bee n. /BEE/ The bee buzzes from flower to flower collecting pollen. Fingerspell BEE
4 Butterfly adv. /BUT-er-fly/ The colorful butterfly was once a caterpillar. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Caterpillar n. /KAT-er-pil-er/ The caterpillar eats leaves before spinning a cocoon. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Ladybug n. /LAY-dee-bug/ The red ladybug has small black spots. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Dragonfly adv. /DRAG-un-fly/ The dragonfly hovers over the pond. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Grasshopper n. /GRAS-hop-er/ The grasshopper jumps through the tall grass. Fingerspell or sign each word
9 Cricket n. /KRIK-it/ The cricket chirps loudly on summer nights. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Spider n. /SPY-der/ The spider is not an insect — it has eight legs. Fingerspell SPIDER
11 Web n. /WEB/ The spider's web sparkles with morning dew. Fingerspell WEB
12 Mosquito n. /mus-KEE-toh/ The mosquito buzzes near your ear at night. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Fly v. /FLY/ A fly landed on the picnic table. Y-hand with index, soar through air
14 Beetle n. /BEE-tul/ The shiny beetle crawls along the branch. Fingerspell BEETLE
15 Worm n. /WURM/ The earthworm helps make the soil healthy. Fingerspell WORM
16 Cocoon n. /kuh-KOON/ Inside the cocoon, the caterpillar transforms. Fingerspell COCOON
17 Hive n. /HYV/ Thousands of bees live together in a hive. Fingerspell HIVE
18 Honey n. /HUN-ee/ Sweet honey comes from the bees' hard work. Fingerspell HONEY
19 Colony n. /KOL-uh-nee/ The ant colony works together underground. Fingerspell COLONY
20 Pest n. /PEST/ A pest is an insect that damages crops. Fingerspell PEST
21 Pollinate n. /POL-ih-nayt/ Bees pollinate flowers so that fruit can grow. Fingerspell or sign each word
22 Larvae n. /LAR-vee/ The larvae of the butterfly look like tiny worms. Fingerspell LARVAE
23 Antenna n. /an-TEN-uh/ The butterfly uses its antenna to sense the world. Fingerspell or sign each word
24 Wing n. /WING/ Each wing of the butterfly is painted with color. Fingerspell WING
25 Magnifying glass n. /MAG-nih-fy-ing GLAS/ Use a magnifying glass to see the tiny details. Fingerspell or sign each word
