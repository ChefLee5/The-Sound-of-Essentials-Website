Scene 5: Hotels & Lodging

After their journey, Ronan and Nerissa check into the Aquaria Inn. Every traveler needs a place to rest.

# Word & Class Pronunciation In the Story… ASL Sign 🤟 My Language
1 Hotel n. /hoh-TEL/ The hotel is near the beach and the train station. H-hand bounces on opposite index finger
2 Motel n. /moh-TEL/ The motel has rooms you can drive right up to. Fingerspell MOTEL
3 Room n. /ROOM/ The room has two beds, a TV, and a bathroom. R-hands form box shape
4 Reservation n. /rez-er-VAY-shun/ Make a reservation before you arrive. Fingerspell or sign each word
5 Check-in n. /CHEK-in/ Check-in time is three o'clock in the afternoon. Fingerspell or sign each word
6 Check-out n. /CHEK-owt/ Check-out time is eleven o'clock in the morning. Fingerspell or sign each word
7 Front desk n. /FRUNT DESK/ Ask for extra towels at the front desk. Fingerspell or sign each word
8 Lobby n. /LOB-ee/ Wait in the lobby while they prepare your room. Fingerspell LOBBY
9 Elevator n. /EL-eh-vay-ter/ Take the elevator to the fifth floor. Fingerspell or sign each word
10 Room key n. /ROOM KEE/ Your room key is a plastic card. Fingerspell or sign each word
11 Housekeeping n. /HOWS-kee-ping/ Housekeeping cleans the room every day. Fingerspell or sign each word
12 Room service n. /ROOM SUR-vis/ Order breakfast through room service. Fingerspell or sign each word
13 Suite n. /SWEET/ The suite has a separate living room and bedroom. Fingerspell SUITE
14 Single room n. /SING-gul ROOM/ A single room has one bed for one person. Fingerspell or sign each word
15 Double room n. /DUB-ul ROOM/ A double room has two beds. Fingerspell or sign each word
16 Vacancy n. /VAY-kun-see/ The sign says "Vacancy" meaning rooms are available. Fingerspell or sign each word
17 No vacancy n. /NOH VAY-kun-see/ "No vacancy" means the hotel is full. Fingerspell or sign each word
18 Swimming pool n. /SWIM-ing POOL/ The hotel swimming pool is open until nine P.M. Fingerspell or sign each word
19 Luggage rack n. /LUG-ij RAK/ Place your suitcase on the luggage rack. Fingerspell or sign each word
20 Complimentary n. /kom-plih-MEN-tuh-ree/ The complimentary breakfast is free for all guests. Fingerspell or sign each word
