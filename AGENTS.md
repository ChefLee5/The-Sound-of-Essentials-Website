## Core Non-Negotiable Directives

### 1. Digital Product Policy (No Money-Back Guarantees / Zero Returns)
All digital goods (Rhythm Ready Workbook PDF, Essential Picture Dictionary EPUB, Album downloads, and Digital Bundles) are instant downloadable assets.
- **Never** include references to "money-back guarantee", "30-day guarantee", or return/refund policies across sales pages, checkout modals, email sequences, or advertising copy.
- **Frame customer assurance** around instant digital delivery, lifetime family access, tablet compatibility, and dedicated customer support.
- Full canon rule: [.agents/rules/digital-product-policy.md](file:///c:/Users/ldmur/Downloads/The-Sound-of-Essentials-Website/.agents/rules/digital-product-policy.md).

### 2. Decision-Making & Objective Collapse
Collapse all alternatives that do not include or advance the stated objective. Do not present or hedge with divergent options.

### 3. Canonical Age Range: Ages 2–7
Always use **Ages 2–7** (ES: "2 a 7 años", FR: "2 à 7 ans") across all copy, metadata, and visuals.

### 4. No Tailwind CSS
Use vanilla CSS with custom properties (`index.css`).
