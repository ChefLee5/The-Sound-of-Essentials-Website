## 🔄 Pedagogical Remodel (Didactic Trap ➔ SOE Sovereign Inquiry)
- **Didactic Habit Replaced:** Mechanical 'keyword-hunting' math worksheets where children blindly add every number they hear ('75 sheep and 5 dogs, how old is the shepherd?').
- **SOE Core Directive:** *Talk FROM, Not ABOUT — Music is the beacon for all children to learn.*

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Step through the Golden Gates of Numeria with Kwame and Octavia to sort true math clues from tricky distractions!

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
92 BPM Detective Clapping Cadence (Track 8 'Numbers'):
• Clap-pat 1-to-10 in crisp unison.
• Teach the Relevance Cue: Give 1 firm stomp for a 'Needed Clue' and wave hands sideways for a 'True-but-Distracting Fact'.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
Kwame's Clue Basket vs. Decoy Basket (7 Minutes):
1. Place 8 wooden blocks, 4 seashells, and 3 blue scarves in the center of the rug, plus two baskets ('Clue Basket' and 'Side-Path Basket').
2. Tell a live tactile story: 'Octavia is building a tower with 4 blocks. She wears 2 blue scarves. Kwame hands her 3 more blocks. How tall is their block tower?'
3. Children physically move the 2 scarves into the Side-Path Basket ('True fact, not a block clue!' — S-31) and stack the 4 + 3 = 7 blocks to a 7-beat drumroll.
4. Test quantifiers (S-10): 'Are ALL things on our rug wooden, MOST, or SOME?' Count to prove it!

---

## 4. 💬 Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
- Relevance (S-31): Why wasn't the number '2 scarves' part of our block answer, even though Octavia really had 2 scarves?
- Measurement Criteria (S-15): If Silas measures our rug with giant boots (6 steps) and Aiko uses tiny steps (12 steps), did the rug grow bigger? How can we make measuring fair?
- Contradiction Check (S-34): Can a group of 5 pebbles be 'more than 6' and 'less than 6' at the exact same time?

---

## 5. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Precise quantifier vocabulary ('all', 'most', 'some', 'none', 'relevant clue').
- **🔢 Cognitive & Critical Logic:** Inhibiting impulsive number-grabbing by isolating relevant mathematical quantities and unit standards.
- **🤸 Physical & Somatic Movement:** Fine-motor block stacking and non-standard foot-to-heel measurement walking.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Inquiry:** Tactile weight, length, and material comparison across wood, shell, and cloth.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Fairmindedness:** Collaborative puzzle-solving where spotting a tricky decoy brings shared laughter instead of test anxiety.

---

## 6. 🌟 Seriphia's Parent / Teacher Whisper (Neuro-Pedagogical Insight)
*Explicitly separating relevant quantities from decoy facts strengthens dorsolateral prefrontal executive filtering, inoculating young minds against mechanical 'plug-and-chug' math habits.*
