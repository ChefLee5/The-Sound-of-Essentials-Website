## 🔄 Pedagogical Remodel (Didactic Trap ➔ SOE Sovereign Inquiry)
- **Didactic Habit Replaced:** Instant learned helplessness ('I can't do it!') or obeying rules only when an adult is watching.
- **SOE Core Directive:** *Talk FROM, Not ABOUT — Music is the beacon for all children to learn.*

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
March with Felix and Amara in Vitalis to turn 'I can't yet!' into steady somatic perseverance and match our promises with our actions.

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
100 BPM Sovereign Cadet Cadence (Track 7 'Drill Time'):
• Bilateral cross-crawl march (right elbow to left knee): 'Stay on the path, always learning!'
• Practice the 5-Second Sovereign Freeze on the drum stop.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Perseverance Bridge & Integrity Mirror (7 Minutes):
1. Challenge pairs to transport a rolling ball or round fruit across the rug balanced on two wooden spoons without touching it with fingers.
2. When it drops (by design!), freeze, place one hand on the heart for 2 slow Amara breaths, and chant: 'Mistakes are clues—how do we adjust our spoons?' (S-8, S-9).
3. Children independently invent a steadier grip or slower pace (S-1) without adult rescue.
4. Reflect honestly on whether anyone nudged the ball with a thumb when nobody looked (S-7, S-27).

---

## 4. 💬 Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
- Perseverance (S-8): When the ball rolled off the spoons the first time, what clue did that drop teach your hands for try number two?
- Ideals vs. Practice (S-27): What is the difference between saying 'I'm sorry' quickly with our mouth vs. actually changing how gently our hands move next time?
- Independent Thinking (S-1): Why does figuring out a balance trick yourself feel stronger than having a grown-up carry it for you?

---

## 5. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Self-coaching metacognitive speech ('Not yet', 'Let's adjust', 'Same fair rule for all').
- **🔢 Cognitive & Critical Logic:** Iterative error-analysis—treating physical drops as diagnostic feedback rather than failure.
- **🤸 Physical & Somatic Movement:** Bilateral cross-lateral marching, visuomotor spoon stabilization, and isometric freeze control.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Inquiry:** Center-of-gravity physics, momentum, and cardiac interoception during breath resets.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Fairmindedness:** Frustration tolerance, intrinsic agency, and honest self-governance.

---

## 6. 🌟 Seriphia's Parent / Teacher Whisper (Neuro-Pedagogical Insight)
*Coupling diaphragmatic vagal braking immediately after a motor error prevents amygdala hijack, wiring the anterior cingulate cortex to treat mistakes as curiosity signals.*
