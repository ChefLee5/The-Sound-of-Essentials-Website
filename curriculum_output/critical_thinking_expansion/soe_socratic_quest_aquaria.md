## 🔄 Pedagogical Remodel (Didactic Trap ➔ SOE Sovereign Inquiry)
- **Didactic Habit Replaced:** Treating emotions as uncontrollable storms disconnected from thoughts, or forcing one-sided 'apologize now' conflict scripts.
- **SOE Core Directive:** *Talk FROM, Not ABOUT — Music is the beacon for all children to learn.*

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Glide with Nerissa and Ronan across the Twin Shores of Aquaria to uncover the hidden thoughts beneath big feelings and see through two sets of eyes.

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
64 BPM Gentle Ocean Wave Sway (Track 14 'The Ocean'):
• Sweep arms in horizontal figure-8 infinity loops across the body midline.
• Inhale 4 counts ('Rising tide'), exhale 6 counts ('/shhhhh/ calm shore').

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Two-Shore Footprint Perspective Swap (7 Minutes):
1. Lay two blue cloth 'shores' facing each other across a ribbon 'river'.
2. Act out a classic everyday moment: Ronan spent 5 minutes building a shell spiral, and Nerissa stepped backward while looking at a butterfly and crushed one edge.
3. First Thought Check (S-4): If Ronan thinks 'She wrecked my shells on purpose!', how does his body feel? Now what if Ronan discovers 'She was looking up and didn't see it!'—how does his feeling change?
4. Children physically step onto Shore A to voice Ronan's care for his work, then cross the midline onto Shore B to voice Nerissa's honest accident, then meet on the bridge to co-rebuild (S-3, S-25).

---

## 4. 💬 Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
- Thoughts & Feelings (S-4): Did the broken shell alone make the angry wave, or did the THOUGHT 'She did it to be mean!' make the wave twice as big?
- Egocentricity Check (S-2): Why is it so easy to notice when someone bumps into US, and so easy to forget when WE bump into someone else?
- Fairminded Resolution (S-3, S-26): What fair next step respects BOTH Ronan's hard work AND the truth that Nerissa didn't mean to step on it?

---

## 5. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Cognitive-affective bridge sentences ('When I assumed ___, I felt ___; when I learned ___, I felt ___').
- **🔢 Cognitive & Critical Logic:** Cognitive reappraisal—testing alternative explanations for peer actions before reacting.
- **🤸 Physical & Somatic Movement:** Horizontal figure-8 midline crossing and spatial perspective-stepping.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Inquiry:** Fluid wave motion, parasympathetic respiratory pacing, and somatic tension release.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Fairmindedness:** Strong-sense fairmindedness, theory of mind, and restorative peer repair.
