## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Kneel on the living earth of Terrasol with Vesta and Silas to test seeds, rain, and stones like true nature detectives!

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
76 BPM Raindrop Finger-Patter (Track 18 'Rain'):
• Patter two fingers softly on the floor like drizzle, build to 8-finger rain, then quiet sun stillness.
• Chant Terrasol's Inquiry Code: 'Observe with senses [1 stomp] — Infer with clues [2 claps] — Test to know [open palms]!'

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Mystery Seed & Sweating Cup Investigation (7 Minutes):
1. Place dry lima beans, small smooth pebbles, and an ice-water cup tinted with 2 drops of berry juice/food coloring on a tray.
2. Intellectual Humility Sort (S-5): Ask 'Are dry beans alive, or non-living like pebbles?' Invite honest 'I'm not sure yet—how could we test it?' Set up two damp-cloth sprout jars (beans vs. pebbles) to observe over 5 days (S-33).
3. Assumption & Inference Test (S-30, S-32): Touch the clear water droplets forming on the outside of the red-tinted ice cup. If the water leaked THROUGH the cup, what color would the outside drops be? Red! Since the drops are clear, where MUST the water have come from? The air around us!

---

## 4. 💬 Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
- Intellectual Humility (S-5): Why does a real scientist love saying 'I don't know yet—let's test!' instead of guessing right away?
- Testing Assumptions (S-30): When people see wet drops outside a cold cup, why do many assume the cup leaked—and how did the red berry tint prove that assumption wrong?
- Evaluating Evidence (S-33): Why do we plant BOTH pebbles and beans in identical wet jars instead of only planting beans?

---

## 5. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Scientific reasoning lexicon ('observe', 'predict', 'evidence', 'condensation', 'living soil').
- **🔢 Cognitive & Critical Logic:** Controlled-variable logic and falsifying intuitive misconceptions through direct counter-evidence.
- **🤸 Physical & Somatic Movement:** Pincer-grasp seed handling and graded tactile fingertip percussion.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Inquiry:** Thermal condensation, botany germination conditions, and mineral vs. organic texture contrast.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Fairmindedness:** Curiosity without fear of being wrong; shared wonder at invisible natural processes.

---

## 6. 🌟 Seriphia's Parent / Teacher Whisper (Neuro-Pedagogical Insight)
*Hands-on controlled contrasts (clear drops outside a colored ice cup; beans vs. pebbles) wire the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex for empirical causality rather than passive rote recall.*
