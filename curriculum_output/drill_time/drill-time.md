# 💪 Track 7: Drill Time
> **The Sound of Essentials: Rhythm Quest** | **Land:** Vitalis (`#c4785a`)  
> **Hero Guides:** Felix & Amara | **Age Range:** Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 1) | **Duration:** 10–15 Minutes

---

## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- Floor tape / chalk line (optional)
- Wooden rhythm sticks or bare hands/feet

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Felix and Amara in Vitalis to form the Sovereign Cadet March and restore focused harmony to our classroom.

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher leads a crisp 100 BPM military-style cadence with chest-pats and foot-stomps:
• Chant: 'Left, left, left-right-left! Wisdom is more precious than gold!'
• Children mirror the exact rhythm with alternating knee-taps while chanting the count '1, 2, 3, 4!'

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Bilateral March & Statue Reset:
1. Children line up along the carpet edge or circle-time rug.
2. Step 1 (March): March in place, lifting knees high to touch opposite elbows (bilateral midline crossing).
3. Step 2 (Shake-Off): On the lyric cue 'We want to shake!', shake left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg for 4 counts each to discharge excess kinetic energy.
4. Step 3 (Sovereign Freeze): On the beat drop, freeze completely still like an ancient mountain in Vitalis for 5 slow breaths.
5. Sit down in unison on the rug without speaking a single word.

---

## 4. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Articulate crisp cadence chant ('Left, left, left-right-left; Sound off! 1, 2, 3, 4!') with vocal projection.
- **🔢 Cognitive & Mathematics:** Auditory response inhibition—processing sudden musical cues to switch from rapid shaking to absolute stillness.
- **🤸 Physical & Motor Skills:** Gross-motor bilateral midline crossing (opposite knee-to-elbow touch) and vestibular balance stabilization.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Exploration:** Acoustic percussion resonance felt through floor vibrations into the feet, grounding kinetic hyperactivity.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Cooperative collective pacing; feeling synchronized pride as a unified circle-time community.
