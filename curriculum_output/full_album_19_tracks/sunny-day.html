☀️ Terrasol 10–15 Minutes

Track 1: Sunny Day

Guides: Vesta & Silas | Target: Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 1)

1. 🎯 Quest Mission

Join Vesta & Silas in Terrasol on an acoustic sensory quest to explore Sunny Day.

📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)

  • Household rhythm sticks or wooden spoons
  • Sensory mat or carpet circle

2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)

Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'Sunny Day', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)

The Hands-On Sensory Discovery: 1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items. 2. Follow the musical cues of Sunny Day to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison. 3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations. 4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

4. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider

🗣️ Language & Phonics Articulate clear auditory phrasing and target vocabulary connected to Sunny Day.
🔢 Cognitive & Math Identify rhythmic patterns, tempo shifts, and conceptual categories in Terrasol.
🤸 Physical & Motor Gross motor whole-body balance, bilateral coordination, and fine motor finger rhythm.
🔬 Science & Sensory Tactile exploration of real-world materials and acoustic auditory pitch discrimination.
🤝 Social-Emotional & Regulation Cooperative participation, shared attentional focus, and calming self-regulation.

5. 🌟 Parent / Teacher Whisper

"Auditory rhythmic pacing stimulates brainstem regulation and synchronizes attention across the classroom without visual screen fatigue."
