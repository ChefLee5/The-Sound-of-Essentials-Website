> **Age Range:** Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 2) | **Duration:** 15 Minutes (4-Step *Rhythm-to-Reason* Ritual)

## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- Household rhythm sticks or wooden spoons
- Sensory mat or carpet circle

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Vesta & Silas in Terrasol on an acoustic sensory quest to explore Changes.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'Changes', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of Changes to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** Gentle 80 BPM Weather-Shift Pulse (Observe What Shifts vs. What Stays Constant)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-5`, `S-18`, `S-30`, `S-32`, `S-33` | **Intellectual Standards:** Consistency, Accuracy, Depth

1. Evaluating Evidence (S-33): The song notices 'It's sunny outside. Yesterday it rained. Somewhere it might snow tomorrow'—what outdoor clues prove weather is always changing?
2. Examining Assumptions (S-30): When a stormy or rainy day arrives, why might we assume the whole day is ruined—and what real-world benefits does changing weather bring?
3. Plausible Inferences (S-32): Why does the song contrast changing weather with unchanging love—what stays steady in a caring family even when moods or seasons shift?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Act out sun, rain, and snow with hands, then hug self for 'steady love'.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Name two things that change every week and two loving promises that stay steady.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Compare evidence of seasonal/weather shifts across places while explaining why constants anchor security.

## 5. 🌐 Step 4: The 5-Domain Spider (2-Minute Whole-Child Closure)
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Articulate clear auditory phrasing and target vocabulary connected to Changes.

- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Cooperative participation, shared attentional focus, and calming self-regulation.
