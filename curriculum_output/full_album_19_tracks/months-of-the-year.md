## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Elias & Selene in Celestia on an acoustic sensory quest to explore Months Of The Year.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'Months Of The Year', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of Months Of The Year to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** 95 BPM Twelve-Step Seasonal Orbit (Connect Months -> Seasons -> Ecosystems)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-11`, `S-12`, `S-13`, `S-23`, `S-35` | **Intellectual Standards:** Completeness, Logic, Significance

1. Interdisciplinary Connections (S-23): Our intro builds from 'seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months'—how do tiny seconds add up to whole seasons of sprouting seeds and falling leaves?
2. Developing One's Perspective (S-12): In January, is it snowy everywhere on Earth, or are children on the other side of our planet having sunny summer?
3. Exploring Consequences (S-35): How do farmers, squirrels, and families plan differently in September and October because they know winter months follow?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Clap through the 12 months and cheer when your birthday month arrives.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Match clothing and tree clues to winter, spring, summer, and autumn months.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Compare seasonal evidence across hemispheres and trace how humans and animals prepare across the 12-month cycle.
