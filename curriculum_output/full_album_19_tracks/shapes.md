## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Vesta & Silas in Terrasol on an acoustic sensory quest to explore Shapes.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'Shapes', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of Shapes to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** Three-Beat Detective Knock (95 BPM: Count the Sides — Test the Rule!)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-5`, `S-18`, `S-30`, `S-32`, `S-33` | **Intellectual Standards:** Precision, Consistency, Accuracy

1. Testing Assumptions (S-30): If we tilt a square onto one corner so it looks like a diamond, is it STILL a square? Let's test our song's rule ('four equal sides' and square corners)!
2. Evaluating Evidence (S-33): How do our fingers prove the difference between a flat circle drawn on paper and a 3D sphere (ball) we can roll across the rug?
3. Evaluating Theories (S-18): Why do honeybees build hexagons and bridges use triangles—what happens when we gently press on a wooden-stick triangle versus a square?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Trace round vs. pointy edges with fingers on real household objects.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Rotate a square block on its corner and count its 4 equal sides to check if its shape changed.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Test structural stability of a triangle vs. square and compare 2D shapes against 3D cubes/spheres.
