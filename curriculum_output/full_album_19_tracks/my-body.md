## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)

- Stickers or soft felt dots

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Team up with Vesta and Silas in Terrasol to map the living architecture of our strong, healthy bodies.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Rhythmic Body Tapping Chant:
• 'What’s on your face? Eyes, nose, mouth, chin!' [Tap each feature with fingertip]
• 'Don’t forget about your forehead, cheeks, and two ears!' [Gentle cup over ears]

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Head-to-Toe Somatosensory Scan:
1. Verse 1 (Face): Children look in mirror or pair with a buddy, touching each facial feature.
2. Verse 2 (Upper Body): Cross arms over chest to tap shoulders ('butterfly tap'), slide down to elbows, wrists, and wiggle all 10 fingertips.
3. Verse 3 (Lower Body): Tap hips, thighs, pat knees, grasp ankles, and stomp 10 individual toes.
4. Bridge (Living Vitality): Inhale deeply, placing right hand on heart to feel the physical cardiac pulse.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** Butterfly Heart-Tap Check (95 BPM: Feel the Pulse — Observe Before Inferring)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-5`, `S-18`, `S-30`, `S-32`, `S-33` | **Intellectual Standards:** Accuracy, Completeness, Logic

1. Evaluating Evidence (S-33): Place your palm over your heart before jumping and after 10 jumps—what direct physical evidence tells you how movement changes your heartbeat?
2. Evaluating Explanations (S-18): Our song says we keep our bodies strong by 'consuming living foods, stretching, and keeping our hearts right'—why do all three work together instead of just one?
3. Plausible Inferences (S-32): If your eyes are closed and you touch your forehead, chin, and elbows, how does your brain still know exactly where each body part is?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Tap eyes, ears, nose, and hands while naming what each sense helps us notice.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Compare heartbeat speed resting vs. marching and report what your hand actually felt.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Connect living whole foods, water, rest, and movement as a testable cause-and-effect health system.

## 5. 🌐 Step 4: The 5-Domain Spider (2-Minute Whole-Child Closure)
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Accurate anatomical vocabulary (chin, forehead, neck, ankles, thighs, living foods).
- **🔢 Cognitive & Mathematics:** Spatial top-to-bottom ordering and bilateral bodily symmetry (left ear vs. right ear).
- **🤸 Physical & Motor Skills:** Proprioceptive tactile mapping and bilateral butterfly self-soothing cross-taps.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Exploration:** Biological sensory perception (heartbeat rhythm, respiratory movement, joint articulation).
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Positive somatic self-image, bodily autonomy, and gratitude for one’s physical temple.
