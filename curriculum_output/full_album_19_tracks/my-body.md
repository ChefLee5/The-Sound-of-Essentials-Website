# 🔬 Track 9: My Body
> **The Sound of Essentials: Rhythm Quest** | **Land:** Terrasol (`#5fb685`)  
> **Hero Guides:** Vesta & Silas | **Age Range:** Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 1) | **Duration:** 10–15 Minutes

---

## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- A hand mirror or partner
- Stickers or soft felt dots

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Team up with Vesta and Silas in Terrasol to map the living architecture of our strong, healthy bodies.

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Rhythmic Body Tapping Chant:
• 'What’s on your face? Eyes, nose, mouth, chin!' [Tap each feature with fingertip]
• 'Don’t forget about your forehead, cheeks, and two ears!' [Gentle cup over ears]

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Head-to-Toe Somatosensory Scan:
1. Verse 1 (Face): Children look in mirror or pair with a buddy, touching each facial feature.
2. Verse 2 (Upper Body): Cross arms over chest to tap shoulders ('butterfly tap'), slide down to elbows, wrists, and wiggle all 10 fingertips.
3. Verse 3 (Lower Body): Tap hips, thighs, pat knees, grasp ankles, and stomp 10 individual toes.
4. Bridge (Living Vitality): Inhale deeply, placing right hand on heart to feel the physical cardiac pulse.

---

## 4. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Accurate anatomical vocabulary (chin, forehead, neck, ankles, thighs, living foods).
- **🔢 Cognitive & Mathematics:** Spatial top-to-bottom ordering and bilateral bodily symmetry (left ear vs. right ear).
- **🤸 Physical & Motor Skills:** Proprioceptive tactile mapping and bilateral butterfly self-soothing cross-taps.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Exploration:** Biological sensory perception (heartbeat rhythm, respiratory movement, joint articulation).
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Positive somatic self-image, bodily autonomy, and gratitude for one’s physical temple.

---

## 5. 🌟 Parent / Teacher Whisper (1 Sentence)
*Cross-body tapping (the butterfly hug) directly calms the amygdala by activating bilateral sensory pathways, reinforcing a grounded and secure internal somatic body schema.*
