# 🌊 Track 14: The Ocean
> **The Sound of Essentials: Rhythm Quest** | **Land:** Terrasol (`#5fb685`)  
> **Hero Guides:** Vesta & Silas | **Age Range:** Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 1) | **Duration:** 10–15 Minutes

---

## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- Household rhythm sticks or wooden spoons
- Sensory mat or carpet circle

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Vesta & Silas in Terrasol on an acoustic sensory quest to explore The Ocean.

---

## 2. 🥁 The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'The Ocean', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of The Ocean to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 🌐 The 5-Domain Spider
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Articulate clear auditory phrasing and target vocabulary connected to The Ocean.
- **🔢 Cognitive & Mathematics:** Identify rhythmic patterns, tempo shifts, and conceptual categories in Terrasol.
- **🤸 Physical & Motor Skills:** Gross motor whole-body balance, bilateral coordination, and fine motor finger rhythm.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Exploration:** Tactile exploration of real-world materials and acoustic auditory pitch discrimination.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Cooperative participation, shared attentional focus, and calming self-regulation.

---
