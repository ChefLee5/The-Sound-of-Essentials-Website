## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- Household rhythm sticks or wooden spoons
- Sensory mat or carpet circle

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Vesta & Silas in Terrasol on an acoustic sensory quest to explore The Ocean.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'The Ocean', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of The Ocean to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** 60 BPM Slow Ocean-Wave Exhale (Listen Deeply Before Speaking)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-5`, `S-18`, `S-30`, `S-32`, `S-33` | **Intellectual Standards:** Depth, Accuracy, Clarity

1. Evaluating Evidence (S-33): As we listen to slow 60 BPM ocean waves, what do you notice happening to the speed of your own breathing and shoulders?
2. Evaluating Explanations (S-18): When we ripple a water tray or jump rope, why do waves look like the water is racing forward even though a floating cork mostly bobs up and down?
3. Intellectual Humility (S-5): Much of the deep ocean has never been seen by human eyes—why does admitting 'We don't know yet' make ocean explorers better scientists?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Sway softly with 60 BPM wave breaths and tap gentle crest-up, trough-down motions.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Watch a floating leaf or cork on rippled water and describe what it actually does.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Connect ocean waves (water energy) with acoustic sound waves (air vibration) using direct observation.
