# ⏰ Track 11: Time

> **Hero Guides:** Elias & Selene | **Sovereign Guardian:** Seriphia  
> **Age Range:** Ages 2–7 (Pre-K to Grade 2) | **Duration:** 15 Minutes (4-Step *Rhythm-to-Reason* Ritual)

---

## 📦 Materials Required (Zero-Prep)
- Household rhythm sticks or wooden spoons
- Sensory mat or carpet circle

---

## 1. 🎯 Quest Mission (1 Sentence)
Join Elias & Selene in Celestia on an acoustic sensory quest to explore Time.

---

## 2. 🥁 Step 1: The Rhythm Ignition (2 Minutes)
Teacher models a steady 2-minute rhythmic call-and-response beat to 'Time', synchronizing clapping and vocal chanting with the children.

---

## 3. 🖐️ Step 2: Tactile Discovery Ritual (7 Minutes)
The Hands-On Sensory Discovery:
1. Children gather in a circle with tactile items.
2. Follow the musical cues of Time to touch, sort, and move in rhythmic unison.
3. Pair high-reach movements with deep somatic exhalations.
4. Freeze in quiet stillness at the final chord.

---

## 4. 💬 Step 3: Seriphia's Socratic Wonder Circle (4 Minutes)
> **Rhythm Cue:** Twilight Pendulum Sway (90 BPM: Morning -> Afternoon -> Evening Ripple)  
> **Wait-Time Rule:** Hold a calm 4-beat pause after every question before inviting responses.  
> **Critical Thinking Strategies Activated:** `S-11`, `S-12`, `S-13`, `S-23`, `S-35` | **Intellectual Standards:** Logic, Relevance, Significance

1. Clarifying Issues & Routines (S-13): Besides looking at clock numbers like 1:00 or 1:30, what light, sky, and body clues tell us whether it is morning, afternoon, or evening?
2. Exploring Consequences (S-35): If we skipped our calm evening wind-down routine and stayed up playing late, what ripple would happen at morning wake-up?
3. Interdisciplinary Connections (S-23): How does keeping steady musical tempo in Harmonia connect to keeping steady daily routines in Celestia?

### Developmental Band Scaffolds (Ages 2–7)
- **Sprouts (Ages 2–4):** Sort 3 picture/object clues into 'Wake-Up Morning', 'Lunch Afternoon', and 'Bedtime Evening'.
- **Pathfinders (Ages 4–5):** Explain which sensory clues (sun height, tummy hunger, yawns) confirm the time of day.
- **Wayfarers (Ages 6–7):** Trace a 3-step morning-to-evening consequence chain and connect rhythmic beat to daily pacing.

## 5. 🌐 Step 4: The 5-Domain Spider (2-Minute Whole-Child Closure)
- **🗣️ Language & Early Literacy:** Articulate clear auditory phrasing and target vocabulary connected to Time.
- **🔢 Cognitive & Mathematics:** Identify rhythmic patterns, tempo shifts, and conceptual categories in Celestia.
- **🤸 Physical & Motor Skills:** Gross motor whole-body balance, bilateral coordination, and fine motor finger rhythm.
- **🔬 Science & Sensory Exploration:** Tactile exploration of real-world materials and acoustic auditory pitch discrimination.
- **🤝 Social-Emotional & Relational:** Cooperative participation, shared attentional focus, and calming self-regulation.
