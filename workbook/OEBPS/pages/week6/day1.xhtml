✨ Hero Quest Moment · with Silas of Terrasol

Silas Builds It Sideways

⏱ 5 minutes with Silas

Pip's birdhouse door faced the ground. Silas opened his mouth to say "That's wrong" — and closed it. "Have you considered how the birds will fly in?" he asked instead. Pip studied it, flipped the birdhouse himself, then added a landing stick Silas had never seen on any birdhouse. "Good thing I didn't fix it," Silas thought. "His way ended up better."

Would you rather someone fix your project FOR you, or ask you a question that helps you fix it yourself? Write why.

✏️ Write your answer here:
Silas's Tip

Silas says: 'Have you considered...' hands someone a tool. 'You're wrong' takes their tools away!
