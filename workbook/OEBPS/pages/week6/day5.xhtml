Athena's Lantern Question

⏱ 5 minutes with Athena

The little inventors' fair was chaos — glue everywhere, ideas half-built, one volcano erupting early. Athena walked the tables with only one sentence: "What would you like to try next?" No fixing, no steering. By day's end, every invention was finished — each one stranger and more wonderful than anything Athena would have designed. That, she wrote in her journal, is the lantern trick: light the path, don't walk it for them.
