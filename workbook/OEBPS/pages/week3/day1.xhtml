✨ Hero Quest Moment · with Kwame of Numeria

Kwame Counts With the Class

⏱ 5 minutes with Kwame

The class had to count all the library books — hundreds of them! Kwame started alone and kept losing track. Then he stopped: "Wait. Let's do this together. WE can each take one shelf." Ten counters finished in minutes what one counter couldn't finish in an hour. The librarian hung a sign: Counted with love, by all of us.

What big job could you finish faster with helpers? Write who you'd invite to your team.

Kwame's Tip
