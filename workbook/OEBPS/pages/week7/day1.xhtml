✨ Hero Quest Moment · with Nerissa of Aquaria

Nerissa's Long Road to R

⏱ 5 minutes with Nerissa

A little Aquarian named Coral couldn't say her R's — "wiver" instead of "river" — and she'd stopped talking in class. Nerissa knelt beside her: "Want to know a secret? Until I was your age, I said 'thquirrel.' Me! The Sound Sculptor!" Coral's mouth fell open. "Sounds take time to sculpt," Nerissa said. "Mine came. Yours is coming." Coral whispered "wiver" — then grinned and tried again.

What's hard for you now that a grown-up you know once found hard too? Ask someone about a thing they struggled with as a kid, and write what they say.

✏️ Write your answer here:
Nerissa's Tip

Nerissa says: Every expert you admire once struggled exactly where you're struggling. The road continues — keep walking!
