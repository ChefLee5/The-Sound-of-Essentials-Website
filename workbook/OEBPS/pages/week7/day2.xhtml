Ronan and the Reader's Story

⏱ 5 minutes with Ronan

Tam hid during reading time — the words swam and scrambled on the page. Ronan sat with him and opened the oldest book in Aquaria's library. Inside the cover, in shaky child-handwriting: 'This book beat me today. — R.' "That's me," Ronan said. "Same book. It beat me forty times before I beat it." Tam looked at the shaky signature, then at the Word Warrior it became. He opened to page one.
