## Small descriptive cards

- **Amethyst:** `石言葉：誠実・心の平和` — “gemstone meaning: sincerity and peace of mind.” `紫色の水晶。2月の誕生石。` — “purple quartz; February birthstone.”
- **Phosphophyllite:** `割れやすく非常に脆い。` — “easily split and extremely fragile.”
- **Opal:** `水分を含んだ宝石。10月の誕生石。` — “a gemstone containing water; October birthstone.”
- **Diamond:** `石言葉：永遠の絆` — “gemstone meaning: eternal bond.” `天然で最も硬い物質` — “the hardest naturally occurring substance.”
- **Sapphire:** `コランダムの変種。9月の誕生石。` — “a variety of corundum; September birthstone.”
- **Tourmaline:** `石言葉：希望・友情` — “gemstone meaning: hope and friendship.” `結晶を熱すると電気を帯びる。` — “the crystal becomes electrically charged when heated.”
- **Ruby:** `石言葉：情熱・純愛` — “gemstone meaning: passion and pure love.” `ダイヤモンドに次ぐ硬度。7月の誕生石。` — “second only to diamond in hardness; July birthstone.”
- **Emerald:** `ベリル（緑柱石）の一種。5月の誕生石。` — “a type of beryl; May birthstone.”
- **Fluorite:** `石言葉：自由・芸術` — “gemstone meaning: freedom and art.” `加熱すると発光する。蛍石。` — “it fluoresces when heated; fluorite.”
