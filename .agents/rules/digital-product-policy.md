# Canon Rule: Digital Product Policy & Guarantee Elimination

## Context & Core Directive
All primary commercial products in **The Sound of Essentials: Rhythm Quest** catalog (including the Rhythm Ready Workbook PDF, Essential Picture Dictionary EPUB, Deluxe Album MP3/lossless downloads, coloring packs, and digital bundles) are **instantly delivered, non-tangible digital assets**.

Per direct founder directive and standard digital goods ecommerce practice:
> **"We want to remove any references to a money-back guarantee. We are selling a product, a digital product, and those don't necessarily come with return policies. So we're going to stay market with that, and make sure we remove any references to a 30-day guarantee or any money-back guarantees."**

---

## Prohibited Terms & Patterns
Across all current and future copy, web pages, checkout funnels, modals, scripts, ad creatives, and customer sequences, the following phrases and concepts are **strictly forbidden**:

- ❌ "30-Day Money Back Guarantee"
- ❌ "100% Money Back Guarantee"
- ❌ "Risk-Free Guarantee" / "Risk-free trial"
- ❌ "30-Day Return Policy" / "Refund policy" offering returns on digital downloads
- ❌ "If you're not satisfied, we'll refund every penny"

---

## Approved Replacement Framing (Value & Assurance)
Customer confidence and conversion trust are established through **product tangibility, delivery immediacy, and lifetime value**:

1. **Instant Digital Delivery**:
   - *"Instant PDF/EPUB download immediately after checkout."*
   - *"Immediate digital access — nothing ships, start today."*

2. **Format & Multi-Device Utility**:
   - *"Tablet-ready & printable format — read on iPad, Kindle, or print at home."*
   - *"Printable activity pages + high-resolution digital master files."*

3. **Ownership & Access**:
   - *"Lifetime family access — download anytime, re-download across devices."*
   - *"Priority digital release access."*

4. **Support & Service**:
   - For customer inquiries or corrupted downloads, direct to customer support: *"Need help with your download? Our support team is ready to assist at support@thesoundofessentials.com."*

---

## Scope of Application
This rule applies to:
- **Web Pages**: `RhythmQuestSale.jsx`, `DictionarySale.jsx`, `Home.jsx`, `ListenExperience.jsx`, checkout modals, and footer fine print.
- **Data & Configs**: `products.json`, pricing tiers, checkout metadata.
- **Marketing & Copy Engine**: Meta ad blueprints, hook archetypes, 5-day email welcome sequences, and press kits.
- **Documentation & Knowledge Items**: Product catalog briefs, revenue stack specifications, and sales funnel guides.
