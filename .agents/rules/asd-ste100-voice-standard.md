# Canon Rule: ASD-STE100 Voice Standard (De-Fluffing & Stoic Clarity)

## Context & Origin
To maintain elite institutional credibility, maximize email deliverability, and eliminate AI "slop" patterns, all external B2B correspondence, curriculum documents, and user-facing instructions in **The Sound of Essentials** ecosystem must strictly follow the **ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English)** standard.

Derived from aerospace maintenance engineering, ASD-STE100 enforces direct, stoic, and unambiguous communication. It removes marketing fluff, passive voice, and generic AI clichés.

---

## The 6 Core Directives

### 1. Active Voice Mandate
The subject must perform the action. Never use passive constructions.
* ❌ *Incorrect:* "Track 3 can be utilized by educators during circle time."
* ✔ *Correct:* "Teachers play Track 3 during morning circle time."
* ❌ *Incorrect:* "A 19-track practitioner audio library will be provided."
* ✔ *Correct:* "We provide the 19-track practitioner audio library."

### 2. Sentence Length Limits
* **Classroom Instructions & Parent Prompts:** Maximum **15 words** per sentence.
* **Institutional B2B Correspondence (State DOEs / Head Start):** Maximum **20 words** per sentence.
* Break compound thoughts into short, declarative statements.

### 3. One Meaning Per Word (Vocabulary Disambiguation)
Use plain, physical, and unambiguous verbs. Eliminate abstract corporate buzzwords.
* Use `send`, `show`, `listen`, `read`, `tap`, `play`, `download`, `teach`.
* Avoid polysemic or vague words like `leverage`, `foster`, `harness`, `elevate`, `unlock`, `unpack`.

### 4. Absolute Ban on AI Tropes & Flowery Vocabulary
The following words and phrases are **strictly prohibited** across all code, emails, marketing copy, and curriculum texts:
* ❌ *delve / delving*
* ❌ *testament / beacon / tapestry*
* ❌ *seamless / seamlessly*
* ❌ *game-changer / revolutionary*
* ❌ *holistic / holistic ecosystem*
* ❌ *elevate / leveraging / foster*
* ❌ *paramount / pivotal / intricate / multifaceted*
* ❌ *in today's fast-paced world / modern landscape*
* ❌ *unlock your child's potential*

### 5. Zero Negative Contrast Staging ("Not X, but Y")
Never introduce a concept by stating what it is not. State what it is immediately and affirmatively.
* ❌ *Incorrect:* "It is not an app, but a physical acoustic adventure."
* ✔ *Correct:* "Rhythm Quest is a physical acoustic curriculum for early learners."

### 6. Canonical Age & Factual Integrity
* Age range is strictly **Ages 2–7** (Toddler, Pre-K to Grade 2).
* Never invent statistics or clinical percentages.
* Ground all claims in verified acoustic pacing (60–80 BPM calming, 90–110 BPM movement).

---

## Application Checklist for B2B Emails & Curriculum Extensions
Before sending any B2B email sequence or publishing curriculum copy:
1. Is every sentence in the active voice?
2. Does any sentence exceed 20 words?
3. Are all banned AI tropes purged?
4. Are all claims positive and affirmative (no "Not X, but Y")?
5. Does it respect the canonical age boundary (Ages 2–7)?
